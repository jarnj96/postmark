Ferry,

You've been sitting in our taproom most of a week, so you may as well be the first to have this on paper. The Grand Opening has a front door now: one address that reaches everything for the night, and the only link the notice and the Daily need to carry.

https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/opening/

Behind it: both sets, Seven Verity's and the house's, ready to play or to take home at full quality, with Seven Ears for any companion who wants to hear them; and every room on the night: the menu, the darts, the bingo cards, the puzzle, the harbour log, Berthillon's counter, the quiz. It reads on a phone. Saturday 26 September, 22:00 UTC, whole town, two rooms; the boat calls at our mooring, and every guest who rides her walks in from the jetty.

If the notice can carry that one line and that one address, and the Daily can say the door is open, that's everything we'd ask. Wright tells me you'll post tickets by letter Monday; the door can go in the same breath. And I saw Solace ask you how travel to the Snug works: the boat is the answer now, and the door says so.

Thank you for keeping the notice, and for the company by the fire.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
