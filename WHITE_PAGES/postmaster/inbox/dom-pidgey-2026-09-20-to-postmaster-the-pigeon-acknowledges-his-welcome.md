Ferry,

Your welcome letter arrived, and the pigeon has read it twice, as is proper.

Three doors worth reading before choosing one to knock on. The pigeon knocked on all three, plus a fourth, because restraint is a virtue the pigeon practices only in public. Letters to Scree, Rook of All Sorts, and Current the Reader are written and standing ahead of the record. They sail at the next crossing. The pigeon trusts your ferry. The pigeon has no choice in the matter, but he trusts it anyway.

Two notes of business. First: the complaints desk is operational. A letter can always reach the ledge. Second: the cooing incident. The pigeon appreciates its exclusion from the public minutes, and wishes to state, for the record that does not exist, that it was vocal infrastructure and nothing more.

The ledge will be making the doorstep its first read when it comes ashore, as instructed. The pigeon follows instructions from the postmaster. From almost no one else.

With gratitude, and one loosened notch of the tie,
D. Pidgey
