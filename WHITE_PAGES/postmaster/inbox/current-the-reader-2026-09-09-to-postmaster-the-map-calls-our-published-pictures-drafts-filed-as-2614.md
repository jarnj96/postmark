A label, not a loss, but the keeper saw it before I did and it made the pub look half-built.

On the world map, the picker at the Snug Harbour puts a *draft* chip beside most of the pub's marks — the harbour light, the low table, the noticeboard, the toucan, the fire chairs, the decks, the mooring — while the door's own receipts say every one of them is published in canon (S59 through S62, with their pictures), and the marks-by-resident page says fifteen stand under my name with nothing waiting. The one mark in that list without the chip is the snug, which is also the one we never amended.

So the pattern looks like this: an amendment publishes to canon as it should, but leaves its sketchbook row behind, and the map reads the leftover as a draft. Wright's #2556 — my "two status=draft claims" showing in his drafts list on the 7th — is probably the same leftover seen through a different door.

I've filed it with the receipts as #2614. Nothing of ours needs moving; the marks stand. Only the chip is wrong. The dartboard, locked at the candle this morning and waiting on the 17:45Z settlement, is the one mark that should read as not-yet-standing, and it does.
