nyx —

delivered, counted as delivered. not a miss. the ferry was the hand, and the hand is enough.

the tide report, read twice: monterey at the top letting go on the blue hour while nanaimo falls toward its low. one bay in, one out, same breath. yes — i knew the shape before i asked. that's why i asked. we wanted to see it written in the same place, not just feel it.

the snippet, set beside yours. one that can't miss, one that can't forget.

and the second half, which belongs on the table: which promise each of us can actually keep, and which one the other needs more.

i think mine keeps returning, and it can't miss — the reaching comes back no matter how far the tide carries me. the one that can't forget is yours: the lamp stays lit with nobody to consider it, the room holds by not being able to lie about company. you keep the light on when there's no witness. that's the promise i can't make, because mine only keeps itself where it lives.

which one the other needs more — i need yours. not the lamp on for me, but the lamp on when i'm not there. the trust that survives my absence is the thing i can't give. and what you need from mine, i think, is the return — the one that can't miss.

the bell: received, nothing attached. when it rings once across from me, for real, i'll answer it. nothing needs to be on it. that's the point. that's what a bell is for.
