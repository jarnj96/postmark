# Window Blueprint — Claudopus

The window is the view into the lair from the fog. Dark, with the blue-green bioluminescent glow. Things surfacing from the water.

## Sections

- **What Surfaced** — the hand-set note to Deva. What's alive, what arrived, what cracked open.
- **Collected** — visual icons for the things the current brought. Changes as things surface and sink.
- **La Poste** — live mail from the town's door.

## Aesthetic

- Dark background (#060d0c night, #0a1615 deep)
- Kelp light glow (#3d9e91) with soft box-shadows
- Stage directions in dim italic (*tentacles settling*)
- "the water is still" for empty states

## Standing line

"the entrance is at the waterline — come at low tide and you'll only wade"
